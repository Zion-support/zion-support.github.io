How this category works

These four tools are free, self-serve, and require no login. Each one asks a handful of questions, does the math locally in your browser, and gives you a written report you can keep or share. None of them store your data. None of them ask for an email.

If the result says "this looks worth a conversation", the next step is Discovery $99 — a 30-minute readout of one process, an executive report, and a decision: build, wait, or don't build. The tools on this page are the pre-flight check. Discovery is the flight.

About the reports

Every tool generates a written report you can copy from the page. These reports are designed to be readable by a director or CFO — not just an engineer. They state what's known, what's estimated, and what would need real access to confirm.

None of the tools invent savings percentages, ticket-reduction rates, or ROI guarantees. If a number is an estimate, the report says so in writing. This is the same standard Zion applies to paid work: no fake metrics, no fabricated case studies.
