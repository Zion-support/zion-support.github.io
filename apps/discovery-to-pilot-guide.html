Six evidence gates

Check a box only when your team has the corresponding written evidence. These are self-attestations: the tool cannot inspect documents or independently verify readiness. Do not enter confidential information.

1. Accountable owner

A named person accepts responsibility for the process, review and next decision.

2. Measured baseline

Record current time, error rate or cost, its measurement period and sample size.

3. Success metric

Define the comparison metric, threshold and review date before testing.

4. Human review

Identify a reviewer, escalation path and prohibited automated actions.

5. Stop and rollback

Set the stop condition, rollback steps and person responsible for reverting.

6. Data permission

Confirm lawful access, minimization, retention and any required security review. Prefer synthetic examples.

Example: a synthetic support-ticket experiment
  1. Start with three synthetic tickets and record manual review time and routing accuracy.
  2. Try a related tool with the same examples; have the named reviewer inspect every output.
  3. Stop if routing is unsafe, confidential data appears or reviewer effort increases beyond your agreed threshold.
  4. Compare the evidence on the review date. Decide build, wait or do not build; do not assume an illustrative Discovery score proves readiness.
