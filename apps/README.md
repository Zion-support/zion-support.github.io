## Conventions

- Every app card links to its **Live** page (https://ziontechgroup.com/<app>/) and its **Repo** (https://github.com/Zion-support/<app>).
- New monthly releases are added to `new<month>-<year>.html` and cross-linked from the showcase and the landing page.
- Suite pages mirror category pages in the hub repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-network/tree/main/network
- Sitemaps: `apps-sitemap.xml` (index) + `apps-sitemap-1..5.xml`.
