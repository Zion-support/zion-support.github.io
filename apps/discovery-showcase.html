Before connecting real customer data

  • Define a baseline: misrouted calls, callback backlog or overdue actions. Choose one success metric and one accountable owner.
  • Use synthetic or consented data first. Confirm recording consent, retention, access permissions and vendor requirements before sharing customer information.
  • Require human review, document escalation and a stop rule, and verify integration behavior before expanding the pilot.

These are workflow exploration examples, not a technical audit, a production integration guarantee or a promise of savings.
