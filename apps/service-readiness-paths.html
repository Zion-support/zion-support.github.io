Choose a connected app path

Field service and smart hands

Write the site scope, time window, required skills, access and safety gates before requesting a technician.

  1. Smart Hands Scheduler — explore a scheduling plan.
  2. SLA Calculator — compare response assumptions.
  3. Supplier Sourcing Control — compare verified costs and authorization gates.

Exit gate: named resource, written availability, all-in quote, exact scope and client confirmation/PO. No automatic booking.

Service desk and IT operations

Use one queue and one owner to test triage without losing human escalation.

  1. Support Ticket Triager — explore categories and routing.
  2. Ops Runbook — document a reviewed response.
  3. Governance Checklist — review permissions and stop rules.

Exit gate: baseline, human reviewer, escalation owner and a reversible test.

Costs and procurement

Compare equivalent scope and currencies. Separate supplier price, delivery and applicable taxes before selecting a route.

  1. ROI Calculator — validate assumptions.
  2. Supplier Risk Radar — organize supplier evidence.
  3. Sourcing Control — preserve margin and PO gates.

Exit gate: verified supplier pricing, documented exclusions, tax/import treatment and formal client approval.

Small automation pilot

Turn the report into a written hypothesis with measurable outcomes instead of a broad platform commitment.

  1. Report Workbench — refine the baseline.
  2. Written Pilot Brief — record owner and scope.
  3. Pilot Evidence Checklist — identify missing evidence.
  4. Pilot Planner — bound the test.

Exit gate: permissions, success metric, human review and stop rule; no implementation authorization inferred.

A written brief your team can verify

EvidenceWhat to recordStop if missing
ScopeProblem, expected outcome, exclusions, site or system and service window.Do not assume a supplier can meet an undefined scope.
ResourceCurrent profile, verified skills, written availability, backup and lead time.Do not advertise unconfirmed availability.
Commercial termsBase price, included hours, minimum charge, travel, freight, after-hours and cancellation.Do not compare incomplete quotes as all-in prices.
Taxes and importJurisdiction, tax treatment, duties and whether quoted sums include them.Confirm with the supplier and qualified tax reviewer; do not invent rates.
AuthorizationFormal written client confirmation, applicable PO and separate Zion authorization.No reservation, purchase or dispatch before these gates.

Do not include passwords, bank details, sensitive client data or technician identity documents in public planning tools. Obtain permission before sharing a supplier profile.
