A reversible pilot checklist

  1. Name one process owner and record a baseline: time spent, error rate or response time.
  2. Choose one app and three synthetic or anonymized examples. Do not upload secrets or confidential client data.
  3. Document permission, human review, acceptance criteria and a stop rule before testing.
  4. Compare the observed result with the baseline. Record limitations and decide to stop, refine or explore implementation.

App pages explain intended use cases; this guide does not certify integrations, model accuracy or production readiness. Validate actual functionality and data handling before use.
