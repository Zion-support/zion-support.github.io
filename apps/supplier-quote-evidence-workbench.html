Supplier Quote Evidence Workbench

Compare two supplier offers for FE visits, hardware or software using the same scope and currency. Calculate complete cost × 1.40 or higher, separate client taxes and expose missing authorization evidence before anyone commits.

Local-only planning. Nothing entered here is uploaded or stored. Use aliases, not confidential client, technician or supplier details. This is a calculator and checklist, not verification of the declarations or a supplier booking system.

First, validate the quote

Check written availability, names, minimum hours, travel, freight, taxes, validity, warranty, payment and cancellation. Market reference prices stay explicitly estimated until a supplier confirms.

Then, protect the order gate

No reservation, purchase or dispatch without written client confirmation, applicable PO and separate Zion authorization. Checking a box here cannot create or replace that evidence.

Comparison settings

Enter zero only when that item is confirmed not applicable. Put supplier taxes in costs only if nonrecoverable. Do not count the same tax twice; client tax is separate and is not profit. No jurisdiction-specific tax rate is supplied by this tool.
