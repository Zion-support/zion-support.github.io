Understand the email status

  1. Report ready: the result is generated locally and displayed immediately.
  2. Submission: the questionnaire requests delivery of the same report to your work email and commercial@ziontechgroup.com, with Carlos copied.
  3. Provider accepted: the email service accepted the request. This does not verify receipt in either inbox.
  4. Inbox received: only a recipient's actual receipt confirms delivery. Check spam and filtering before resubmitting.

If acceptance is unavailable or times out, keep the on-screen result, copy/download it, or use the manual email link. A timeout can occur after a request has been accepted; check your inbox before sending again.

Hosting and email services can fail. Free and online does not imply guaranteed uninterrupted uptime or immediate inbox arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy something or book a meeting?

No. Discovery and access to your report are free. Any paid engagement requires a separate agreement.

What data should I enter?

Use high-level process information. Do not enter passwords, bank details or confidential client data. Submission uses a third-party email service to reach the listed recipients.

Does a recommendation mean the app is production-ready?

No. Evaluate actual functionality, data handling and integration requirements before using an app in production.

Can Discovery authorize hardware purchases or engineer dispatch?

No. Written scope, formal client confirmation, PO and Zion authorization are required before any real purchase, reservation or dispatch.
