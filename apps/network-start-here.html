Four questions before choosing an app

Compare one workflow using evidence, not an app count.

  1. What actually runs?

    Separate a documented concept, a local browser tool and a verified integration. Inspect the linked source and record what you tested.

  2. Which inputs are safe?

    Start with synthetic examples. Identify personal, confidential or regulated data and obtain the required review before using real records.

  3. What will you measure?

    Record the current process, one baseline and one success criterion. Estimated savings are assumptions until measured.

  4. Who can approve or stop?

    Name a human owner, define a stop rule and confirm permissions before a pilot. A guide is not authorization to purchase, send email or dispatch work.
