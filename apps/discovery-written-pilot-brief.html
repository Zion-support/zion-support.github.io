Take your free Discovery report one step further. Document one process, a baseline, a measurable success target, human review and a stop rule before trying automation. Keep decisions in writing without requiring a meeting.

Why begin with a written brief?

Compare before buying

Record the same baseline and goal for every candidate app. Compare human effort, error rate and risk, not unsupported ROI promises.

Make a small trial reversible

Use fictional or approved sample data. Keep human review, a named owner and an explicit stop condition.

Keep the decision documented

Download a text brief for written review. Optional paid implementation stays separate; hardware and site work require formal approval and a PO.
