Your first-week checklist

  1. Choose one repetitive process and confirm its owner.
  2. Measure volume, handling time and exception rate.
  3. Confirm data permissions and define human review.
  4. Select a reversible pilot and an explicit stop rule.
  5. Compare the pilot with the baseline before expanding.

Use a success metric such as correctly routed requests or minutes of review per case, not an unsupported promise of revenue or savings. This guide creates no engagement, order, supplier booking or dispatch.
