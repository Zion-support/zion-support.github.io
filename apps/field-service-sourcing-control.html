Field Service & Supplier Sourcing Control

A free, local pre-commitment tool for FE, smart-hands, hardware and software sourcing. Verify written costs before releasing a client quote. Nothing here sends email, stores your answers, selects a supplier, places an order or dispatches a technician.

Updated calculation: negative or malformed amounts are rejected. Sell prices round up to the next cent so rounding cannot reduce the minimum 40% markup.

1. Calculate the minimum sell price

Use the same currency for every item. Enter amounts without separators, using a decimal point and at most two decimal places. Maximum per amount: 999999999.99.

Include travel, freight, insurance, handling, parking and nonrecoverable local/import taxes once. Do not count a charge both here and in client taxes below.

Enter the independently verified client-tax amount. This tool does not determine VAT, sales-tax, import liability, recoverability or exemptions. Unknown tax treatment means HOLD, not zero tax.

Enter verified costs. An unverified market estimate is planning-only, never a confirmed supplier quote.

2. Evidence gates

Checking boxes is a self-assessment, not proof or authorization. Keep the underlying documents and re-read the current thread.

HOLD: do not reserve, purchase, book, travel, dispatch or incur supplier cost.

If a PO does not apply, document that exception and have the authorized Zion owner review it. This app cannot waive approval gates.

3. Compare commercial terms, not just the lowest rate

  • Written scope, skills/certifications, OEM warranty/support, included hours and minimum charge.
  • Quote validity, delivery lead time, cancellation/rescheduling, payment terms and currency.
  • All-in economics with at least 40% markup; 40% markup is approximately 28.57% gross margin before overhead, not 40% margin.
  • Separate local taxes, import costs, freight and travel; distinguish borne costs from legally charged client taxes.
  • Client RFQ/proposal in the existing thread, with the agreed scope and separately reviewed PDF. Keep supplier-cost calculations in the internal Commercial record, not the client-facing quote.
  • One opportunity owner. Check Sent/thread history across agents before replying; no more than one unanswered follow-up per seven full days.

For a public-market estimate, record source, date, currency, assumptions and uncertainty. Obtain written supplier terms before selecting a partner or promising availability. No market average guarantees profit or stock.
