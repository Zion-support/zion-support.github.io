Four questions worth testing

Choose the question that matches your available data and your team’s responsibility. Each card opens the source concept and identifies an evaluation goal.

Documented concept

Demand forecasting

Can a forecast improve the way your team plans for electricity demand?

Evidence to prepare

Use historical demand, weather and calendar data with consistent timestamps. Compare against a simple baseline on an unseen time period; record typical and peak-demand errors.

Documented concept

Outage triage

Can better event grouping help a dispatcher review an incident faster?

Evidence to prepare

Use anonymized historical events and confirmed incident outcomes. Measure triage time and incorrect priorities; keep dispatch, site access and safety decisions with the authorized operator.

Renewable performance

Which production losses deserve investigation first?

Evidence to prepare

Gather production, weather, downtime and curtailment records. Compare equivalent operating conditions and review the assumptions with an energy specialist before changing equipment settings.

Documented concept

Meter anomaly review

Which readings need a human check before a billing decision?

Evidence to prepare

Review approved, anonymized meter histories and known data-quality issues. Track false alerts and confirmed findings; an unusual reading alone does not establish fraud or justify a billing change.

Turn a concept into a bounded pilot

  1. 01

    Define one decision

    Choose a site or process, a responsible owner and a measurable outcome.

  2. 02

    Confirm usable data

    Document the source, period, units, permissions and missing values. Start with an approved sample.

  3. 03

    Set a fair comparison

    Agree on a baseline, evaluation period and success threshold before inspecting the results.

  4. 04

    Review before acting

    Test recommendations alongside the current process. Record failures and decide with the responsible team whether to continue, revise or stop.
