FAQ

How do I pay for Discovery?

Use the hub /discovery/ or the official Payment Link: $99 USD (ZG0b). For invoice paperwork, use /contact/.

Discovery vs Consulting vs Starter vs Growth

Discovery maps 1 process and returns a report + session — it does not implement. Consulting deepens strategy and roadmap (3 sessions). Starter implements 1 automation with 30 days support. Growth is a monthly retainer with ongoing operations and SLA — usually scoped after Discovery. Theme detail: AI consulting, FinOps, agents, managed IT.

What happens after payment

We receive the Stripe notice and reply within 24 business hours (email / WhatsApp / contact) to collect the process, schedule the session, or confirm kickoff. Discovery: map + report + session within 7 days. Consulting / Starter / Growth: the team confirms scope and books. If you paid the Growth retainer without a prior Discovery, the operating cut still closes with the team — it is not a generic retainer without diagnosis.
