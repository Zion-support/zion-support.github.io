## Rules
1. Every public showcase/spotlight ships in all 5 languages at release.
2. Naming: `<page>.html` (en) + `-pt`, `-es`, `-fr`, `-de` suffixes.
3. `<html lang>` per variant + hreflang alternates to all variants.
4. Product names stay English; value props, CTAs, Discovery block and nav are translated.
5. Language switcher on top of every page: 🌐 EN · PT · ES · FR · DE.
