# 🌱 NEW: Sustainability & ESG AI Suite — Zion App Network Spotlight (2026-10-03, Batch 68)

**Zion Tech Group** just interlinked six AI apps into a complete sustainability loop: **measure → audit → optimize → forecast → balance → report**. Every app is live in the [Zion App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/app-network/) and open on GitHub.

| Stage | App | What it does |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| 1. Measure | [Carbon Footprint Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/carbon-footprint-tracker) | Scope 1–3 emissions baselines from energy, travel & procurement data |
| 2. Audit | [Energy Audit Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-audit-copilot) | Meter anomaly detection, savings estimates, capex-ranked measures |
| 3. Optimize | [Energy Optimizer AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-optimizer-ai) | HVAC/lighting schedules, demand response, tariff-aware load shifting |
| 4. Forecast | [Renewable Energy Forecaster](https://github.com/Zion-support/renewable-energy-forecaster) | Solar/wind generation forecasts driving clean-energy scheduling |
| 5. Balance | [Smart Grid Load Balancer](https://github.com/Zion-support/smart-grid-load-balancer) | Multi-site load & storage coordination, peak shaving, self-consumption |
| 6. Report | [ESG Report Builder](https://github.com/Zion-support/esg-report-builder) | CSRD/GRI/TCFD-ready reports with auditor evidence links |

## Why now

- **CSRD deadlines are here** — automate evidence collection instead of spreadsheet season.
- **Energy costs are volatile** — the optimizer + forecaster pair cuts peaks when prices spike.
- **Governance built-in** — pairs with [Zion AI Governance](https://governance.ziontechgroup.com) for EU AI Act-aware operations.
