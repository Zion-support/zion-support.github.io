## How the apps interlink

1. [Onboarding Journey Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/onboarding-journey-tracker) feeds activation data into [Customer Health Scorer](https://github.com/Zion-support/customer-health-scorer).
2. [Support Sentiment Monitor](https://github.com/Zion-support/support-sentiment-monitor) streams sentiment risk into [Churn Risk Radar](https://github.com/Zion-support/churn-risk-radar) and [Churn Radar](https://github.com/Zion-support/churn-radar).
3. Healthy accounts surface in [Upsell Signal Scout](https://github.com/Zion-support/upsell-signal-scout) and feed partner motions in [Partner Referral Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/partner-referral-tracker).
4. [NPS Insight Engine](https://github.com/Zion-support/nps-insight-engine) and [Voice of Customer Hub](https://github.com/Zion-support/voice-of-customer-hub) close the loop back into [Support Deflection Analyzer](https://github.com/Zion-support/support-deflection-analyzer).
5. Ticket-heavy accounts route to [Zion AI Ticket Sentinel](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-ticket-sentinel/) and [SLA Breach Predictor](https://ziontechgroup.com/sla-breach-predictor/).
