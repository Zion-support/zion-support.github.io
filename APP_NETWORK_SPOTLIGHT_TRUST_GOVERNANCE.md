## How the apps interlink

1. Start with the [AI Governance Policy Generator](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-policy-generator) to set the framework, then operationalize meetings with [Meeting Policy Gen](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-meeting-policy-gen).
2. Run high-risk processing through the [DPIA Assistant](https://github.com/Zion-support/dpia-assistant); retention outcomes feed the [Data Retention Enforcer](https://github.com/Zion-support/data-retention-enforcer).
3. When incidents happen, [Zion AI Postmortem](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-postmortem) turns them into action items that update policies.
4. Customer-facing proof: [Vendor Questionnaire Autopilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-questionnaire-autopilot) reuses your policies, and [Accessibility Statement Gen](https://github.com/Zion-support/accessibility-statement-gen) covers public commitments.
5. Keep humans in the loop with [Security Awareness Coach](https://github.com/Zion-support/security-awareness-coach).
