# Security & privacy

This is a static browser-only analysis tool: no login, server, API, tracking, analytics, or telemetry. Inputs are processed in memory and never intentionally transmitted or stored. Do not paste secrets, personal data, customer RFQs, raw prompts or confidential production traces without permission. Download actions are user-triggered. The browser, any extensions, and external domains linked in navigation have their own policies.

Tool outputs are advisory only: do not use them as proof of certification, contractual SLA, engineer availability, approval, or compliance.

If you find a vulnerability, contact commercial@ziontechgroup.com privately; do not submit customer information in public issues.
