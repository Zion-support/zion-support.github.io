# Competitor Landscape — AI Consulting in Brazil

**Prepared for:** Zion Tech Group — Market positioning analysis
**Date:** September 2026
**Scope:** 5-8 competitors identified; pricing comparison; differentiation gaps; partnerships

---

## Executive Summary

Brazil's AI consulting market is fragmented across three tiers: Big Four advisory houses, global systems integrators with local arms, and specialist boutiques. Zion's Discovery→Consulting→Starter→Growth funnel is unique in the Brazilian market — most competitors offer either strategy-only or full implementation, with a gap in the low-cost, structured entry point that Zion fills at $99 Discovery / $499 Consulting.

**Key finding:** No Brazilian competitor offers a self-serve, USD-denominated, 231-country field-services rate card combined with AI consulting. This is Zion's white-space opportunity.

## Differentiation Opportunities — What Others Are NOT Doing

### 1. Self-Serve, USD Rate Card Across 231 Countries
Nobody else offers a published, self-serve rate card for field services across 231 countries with Stripe checkout. This is Zion's single biggest structural advantage. Global SIs price by RFQ; Zion prices by SKU.

### 2. Ultra-Low Discovery Price ($99)
The market standard for AI readiness assessment is $15K-60K. Zion's $99 Discovery captures SME and mid-market clients that the Big Four ignore and boutiques can't reach at that price point.

### 3. Portuguese-Language AI Consulting at Global Scale
CI&T and Stefanini are Brazilian but serve Brazil primarily. Zion's Portuguese-native team (Brazil + Portugal + Angola + Mozambique) can serve the Lusophone world with AI consulting — a market no global SI adequately covers.

### 4. Field Services + AI Consulting Combo
Most AI consultancies don't do field services. Most field services companies don't do AI. Zion's combo (Discovery→Consulting→Starter→Growth + global field services) is unique.

### 5. CPA / Partner-Led Growth Model
Zion's referral/CPA model (pay per result, not per hour) is uncommon in Brazilian consulting, where hourly billing dominates. This aligns incentives with client success.

### 6. Stripe-Powered Checkout
No Brazilian AI consultancy offers Stripe checkout for service SKUs. This signals maturity and reduces sales friction.
