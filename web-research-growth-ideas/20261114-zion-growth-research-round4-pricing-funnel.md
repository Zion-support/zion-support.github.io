# 2026-11-14 — Research round 4: AI services pricing & delivery funnel

Sources (fact-only; no invented ROI/savings/pricing):
- Farasat Ali, "AI Consulting Services: Scope, Prices, Red Flags" — 3 vendor guides: assessments $10K–$75K; retainers $2K–$50K/mo; independents $150–$350/hr; engagement bands from ClearForge (diagnostic $10K–$25K; strategy/roadmap $25K–$75K; POC $50K–$150K; implementation $150K–$500K; managed ops retainer $5K–$25K/mo).
- taskip.net, "AI Automation Agency Pricing 2026" — fixed $3K–$20K; usage $80–$300/unit; subscription+usage $1.5K–$4K/mo; outcome $1K/mo + per result; productized $1.5K–$5K/mo; hybrid $2K–$6K/mo. Mid-market: setup $5K–$20K one-time, retainer $1.5K–$4K/mo. Productized packages land $1,800–$4,500/mo.
- clientvenue.com, "AI Agency Pricing" — AI readiness assessment $5K–$25K; POC $15K–$75K; full SMB implementation $30K–$150K; enterprise $150K–$500K+; ongoing monitoring retainer $3K–$20K/mo; AI training $10K–$50K; AI strategy $15K–$60K.
- automatonagency.com — small business: project $1.5K–$5K, retainer $500–$1.5K; mid-market: project $5K–$25K then embedded partnership $3K–$8K/mo; enterprise $20K–$100K+ then $3K–$8K/mo.
- agencypro.app — project $15K–$75K standard; strongest transition to $5K–$20K/month retainers within 6 months; model table: Strategy $15K–$60K/$4K–$10K/mo; Implementation $20K–$120K/$5K–$25K/mo; Enhanced Delivery $5K–$40K/$3K–$15K/mo; maintenance $2.5K–$5K/mo, growth $5K–$12K/mo, strategic $12K–$25K/mo; RAG implementation starter $25K–$45K.

Key takeaways for Zion:
- Mid-market AI services: fixed setup $5K–$20K, monthly retainer $1.5K–$4K, hybrid/outcome-based.
- Funnel: free Discovery → strategy/roadmap sprint → proof of concept → implementation → managed operations retainer.
- Variants: Strategy ($4K–$10K/mo), Implementation ($5K–$25K/mo), Enhanced Delivery ($3K–$15K/mo), maintenance ($2.5K–$5K/mo), growth ($5K–$12K/mo), strategic ($12K–$25K/mo).
- Productized packages land $1.8K–$4.5K/mo; strong agencies transition within 6 months.
