AR Collections Copilot

A proposed accounts-receivable review workflow: aging analysis, follow-up planning and promise-to-pay tracking with human approval.

What is available today?

The linked repository contains documentation, not an implemented collections engine. This page is informational. Production readiness, banking/ERP integrations, SSO, RBAC, encryption and automated messaging have not been established here.

Do not use this concept as legal advice, a credit decision, permission to contact debtors or approval to send collection messages automatically.

Define a responsible pilot

  1. Identify the receivables owner, applicable rules and the approved communication process.
  2. Use synthetic or de-identified invoices and reconcile balances against a checked baseline.
  3. Define aging buckets, exceptions and human review before any follow-up.
  4. Measure reconciliation accuracy and exception handling, not only response rates.
  5. Stop on disputes, inconsistent balances or missing permissions. No automated external contact.
