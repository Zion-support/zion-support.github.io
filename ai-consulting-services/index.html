Readiness checks before spend

Readiness is a checklist on this page — not a separate /ai-readiness-assessment/ SKU. If two or more items below are missing, start with Discovery $99, not a platform purchase.

  1. Named process — one workflow with a start, a system of record, and a definition of done. “We need AI” is not a process.
  2. Named owner — a human who can pause the work, approve a write, and take the readout to finance.
  3. Data boundary — which systems and fields are in scope. Unsanctioned paste-into-chat is out until a control owner says otherwise.
  4. Baseline — a number you already track, or a measurement window Discovery will write. No invented KPI.
  5. Human gate — irreversible or high-cost actions wait for a person. If you cannot name the gate, you are not ready for an agent.
  6. Cost envelope — what you will spend on people, tools, and model calls before the next review. Growth $8,000/mo is a managed loop, not a blank check.
  7. Kill rule — what “stop” looks like: no owner, no baseline movement, or spend without a log.

Healthcare or PHI processes start on healthcare IT. Payments or banking-adjacent stacks start on industry solutions. This page does not sell a certified readiness product.

FAQ

Is this the same as the AI services hub?

No. /services/ is the catalog hub. This page is the commercial landing for “AI consulting services” — outcomes, ROI order, and the path to a project or retainer.

When should we hire consulting vs start an agent?

If you already know the process and the KPI, autonomous agents may be the next conversation. If you do not, start here or on Discovery.

How do you treat healthcare or regulated processes?

Discovery is compliance-oriented. When the process needs healthcare-grade handling, we treat it as HIPAA-ready: named owners, data boundaries, and human approvals. That is operating language, not a product badge.

What does Discovery include?

One process, an ROI sketch, an executive report, and a 30-minute session. Pay on /discovery/ (BRL ZG09 · USD ZG0b) or start from /contact/ for invoice.

How fast do you respond?

Within 24 hours via /contact/ or kleber@ziontechgroup.com.

How do you present ROI to a board?

One process, a baseline you already measure (or a measurement window), impact × effort rank, and a cost envelope. Discovery $99 is the diagnostic. We do not invent a payback percentage or a case study. See Board-level ROI and Pay vs Consulte for AI automation.

Do you sell a separate AI readiness assessment?

No. There is no /ai-readiness-assessment/ URL. Readiness is the checklist on this page. If the process, owner, baseline, or kill rule is missing, start with Discovery $99.

What if we are not ready to spend on a build?

That is a valid Discovery outcome. We will recommend consulting, a smaller automation, waiting, or pairing with FinOps when the bill is the constraint. Do-not-build is a deliverable, not a failed sale.

When does consulting become an agent project?

When the process, owner, data boundary, and human gate are written. Then the next conversation is autonomous AI agents — still sequenced Discovery → Starter → Growth. Governance and helpdesk deepen that page; they are not separate SKUs.
