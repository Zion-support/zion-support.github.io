# Zion App Network Spotlight — Batch 50: FinOps & Cloud Cost Optimization (Sep 26, 2026)

Eight interlinked apps that form one FinOps toolchain: **estimate → analyze → optimize → automate → prove**.

- **[AI FinOps Suite](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-finops-suite/)** — Unified FinOps command center: multi-cloud cost visibility, budgets, anomalies, commitments and chargeback in one AI suite. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-finops-suite))
- **[FinOps Spend Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/finops-spend-analyzer/)** — Deep-dive cost analysis: breaks cloud spend by service, team and tag, finds idle resources and rightsizing wins. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/finops-spend-analyzer))
- **[FinOps Autopilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/finops-autopilot/)** — Automated cost governance: policy-driven actions that stop waste — schedules, cleanup and commitment management. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/finops-autopilot))
