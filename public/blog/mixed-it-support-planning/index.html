Infrastructure planning guide

Planning support for mixed IT environments

Bring servers, operating systems and networks into one written scope before requesting support or planning a site visit.

A historical starting point

Zion's past service experience included hardware and software support across Windows, Linux, HP-UX, Solaris, AIX and network environments. This guide turns that historical scope into questions for a new engagement. Current needs, technical qualifications and resource availability must be assessed for each request.

Prepare a useful support brief

Describe the environment

List platform families, operating-system versions and the role of each affected service. Use a high-level inventory in the first exchange; credentials and detailed access information are not needed.

Describe the task and impact

State what changed, the symptoms or planned work, and how the issue affects the business. Distinguish diagnosis, repair and a proposed improvement so each can be scoped.

Map responsibilities

Identify the system owner, existing support arrangements, access-approval process and dependencies between servers, applications and network equipment.

Agree the work and the evidence

Before any intervention

  1. Choose a service window and timezone, identify constraints, and define how to restore the previous state if needed.
  2. Confirm the technical scope, resource qualifications and responsibility for any parts, licences or vendor escalation.
  3. On-site attendance and purchases require written client confirmation and a purchase order.

For the completion record

  1. Record the checks performed and their findings against the agreed scope.
  2. Separate completed work from unresolved issues and proposed next steps.
  3. Ask the client to confirm the result in writing, including any agreed limitations or remaining actions.

Choose one next step

Start with one system or support workflow. A first assessment can identify missing information and the boundaries of a possible engagement; delivery scope, coverage and service levels are agreed separately.

Start with free online Discovery

Use the free Discovery questionnaire to describe the objective and initial scope. You can begin online and continue by email; a meeting is not required to start. The Apps catalog also offers planning resources for the next step.
