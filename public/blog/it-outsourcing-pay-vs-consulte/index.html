What this page is (and is not)

This is a fit guide for the commercial shape of IT outsourcing — Consulte to name outcomes before paper, Discovery to frame owners, and Pay only if a slice is already bounded. It is not a case study and it does not invent a cost-takeout percentage, headcount cut, SLA letter grade, or certified outcome. Published commercial prices live on /plans/. Scope risk of a blanket MSA

A blanket MSA feels like progress because a vendor is “on contract.” The usual failure is commercial, not technical:

  • Hours without outcomes. The paper buys capacity. Nobody wrote what “good” looks like for a named tower.
  • Vendor becomes the strategy. Keep-vs-send was never decided, so every new request lands on the MSA by default.
  • No exit. Knowledge, admin rights, and runbooks stay with the vendor because the SOW never named an acceptor on your side.

That is why this page leans Consulte. Signing first and scoping later is how blanket MSAs expand. Consulte names outcomes, owners, and edges. Discovery $99 is the cheap written frame when you are not ready for a SOW conversation. Starter ZG06 can follow only as a bounded execution slice — one queue, one runbook, one control — never as “we outsourced IT for $2,500.”

What Discovery $99 writes

  • Owners, systems, and keep-vs-send for this conversation — not a company-wide outsourcing program.
  • A recommendation: Consulte a SOW, a later Starter slice, Growth/retainer, or stop.
  • The stop rule above, in writing, so a checkout cannot be treated as a blanket MSA.

Discovery is not implementation and it is not a substitute for Consulte on a multi-tower deal. Implementation of a named slice is Starter or a scoped SOW. Ongoing coverage is Growth after the model is clear. Desk-level slice language: IT service desk automation Pay vs Consulte.

Good after kickoff (no fake math)

  • The outcome sentence still matches day 10 — same tower, same keep-vs-send, same acceptor.
  • Access landed or a named blocker with an owner. A vendor without your identity owner is not “done.”
  • Definition of done is operational — a live path, map, or control — not “we signed the MSA.”
  • Exception queue exists. Humans know what the vendor must not decide alone.

If the conversation slid from one slice to “all of IT,” stop and re-scope. That is Consulte, not a silent expand of Pay.

FAQs

Can I skip Consulte and Pay Starter ZG06 for outsourcing?

Only if a single execution slice is already named (one queue, one runbook, or one control), systems are reachable, and someone will accept “done.” Starter is never a blanket MSA. If the job is “outsource IT,” start at /contact/ or map first with Discovery $99.

Is Consulte a free consultation?

No. Consulte is a human-scoped conversation via /contact/ when the SKU is unclear or the paper would be a blanket MSA. Discovery is the $99 written diagnostic. There is no free consultation SKU.

Do you publish cost-takeout, headcount, or ROI percentages for outsourcing?

No. This guide uses fit patterns only. Use an operating signal you already measure. Board language without fake math starts from the Consulte SOW or the Discovery map.

Where do Consulting $499 and Growth fit?

Consulting $499 sits between Discovery and a SOW when you need RACI or roadmap without a vendor paper yet. Growth $8,000/mo is ongoing coverage after the model is clear — still not a blanket MSA. Ladder: /plans/.
