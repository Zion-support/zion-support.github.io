What is the Zion App Network?

For over two decades, Zion Tech Group has delivered field services, hardware, and IT operations for enterprises worldwide. Along the way, we built internal tools to solve recurring problems: estimating cloud costs, auditing AI compliance, planning site surveys, optimizing dispatches, comparing LLM pricing.

We turned each of those solutions into a focused micro-app — and released all of them, free and open source. Every app is live on ziontechgroup.com and open on our GitHub organization, and every app cross-links its siblings, so you can discover related tools in one click.
