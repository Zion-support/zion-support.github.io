AI security practices that stay on the page

Name the system, the data it can see, and who stops a run. This note does not list attacks and it does not ask you to paste a secret.

Write these four lines

  • Owner of the system and the person who can shut it off.
  • What the model is allowed to read, and what it must not send out.
  • Where evidence of a bad answer is stored.
  • The date you will review the scope again.
