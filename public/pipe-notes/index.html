Pipeline notes

A written path for a data job, a name check, or a timeline. Nothing on this page is probed, scanned, or sent.

What to write down

  • The pipeline name, the contract it should match, and who signs off.
  • The certificate or name you are checking. Use the certificate checklist.
  • The incident timeline stays in the runbook notes. Do not open a github.io copy.
