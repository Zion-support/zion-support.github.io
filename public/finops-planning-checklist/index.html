A practical checklist before your FinOps review

Start with one decision, not another dashboard. This educational guide connects the public planning tools; it does not inspect cloud accounts, audit invoices or guarantee savings.

Four decisions to write down

  1. Name one owner

    Record the person who can approve a change, the workload in scope and who can stop or reverse the pilot. A tool cannot supply missing authority.

  2. Establish a comparable baseline

    Use the same period, workload and definition of completion. Record spending and completed work separately. Without a baseline, do not invent a savings percentage.

  3. Check reliability and governance together

    Record service targets, outage assumptions, accountable owners and missing control evidence together. A planning checklist is not a service contract, security audit or certification.

  4. Choose a small next step

    Agree the question, evidence and access boundaries for a small review first. Any later paid service is optional and must be scoped separately.

Keep the denominator honest

Cost per completed workflow = period spending ÷ completed workflows

If no workflows completed, do not divide by zero: record the spending and the missing outcome separately.
