Recommendation note

The older page described a live ranking engine. This page does not rank a catalog, does not read a session, and does not show a product to a customer.

Write down

  • The catalog you already sell, and the one signal you trust (a past order, a search, or a category).
  • What must never be suggested (a regulated item, an out-of-stock line, a price you have not approved).
  • The person who approves the list before it is shown.

A note is not a recommendation. Discovery maps whether a ranking pilot is even the right next step.
