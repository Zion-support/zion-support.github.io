Composio notes

A connector note you fill in yourself. Nothing is connected, authorized, or called from this page.

What to write down

  • The apps the team already uses, and the person who owns each login.
  • Which actions a workflow may take, and which ones stop for a person.
  • Where the log of a run will live. This page does not store one.
  • The comparison notes if you are choosing a workflow host. They do not rank a product.
