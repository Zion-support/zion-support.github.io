Scope notes

These pages replace older addresses that still serve yesterday's HTML, including links to hosts that do not open. Each note is words on the page. Nothing is sent, scored, uploaded, or filed.

  • SEO brief — Write the query, the page, and the proof before anyone talks about rank. This page does not rank a URL.
  • Engagement notes — Notes a manager writes about a team. This page does not send a survey.
  • Obligation notes — Dates and owners from a contract you already have. This page does not file anything and is not legal advice.
  • Renewal notes — The renewal date and the person who decides. This page does not scan an inbox.
  • Sales email notes — The email a person will send. This page does not send it.
  • Vendor notes — Questions to answer before a purchase. This page does not score a vendor.
  • Policy notes — An outline for a policy a person will write. This page is not legal advice and does not publish a policy.
  • Knowledge notes — The article you would actually publish. This page does not write it into a knowledge base.
  • Dispatch notes — The window and the owner for one visit. This page does not dispatch anyone.
  • Asset notes — What you own, where it sits, and when the warranty ends. This page does not scan a network.
  • Staffing notes — The seats you need and who covers them. This page does not price a team.
  • Warranty notes — The warranty date and the owner. This page does not look up a serial number.
  • SLA notes — What was promised, and what a person will say if it is missed. This page does not forecast a breach.
