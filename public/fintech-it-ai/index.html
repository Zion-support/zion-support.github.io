Controls before agents

Fintech and finance-adjacent teams fail AI projects when autonomy ships ahead of controls:

  • Unclear who may grant system access for agents or copilots
  • No change control for workflows that touch money movement, KYC/AML tooling, or customer data
  • Model/API spend without budgets or owner tags
  • Vendor questionnaires answered with marketing claims instead of posture

Zion’s default: map controls and ownership first, then automate. Discovery diagnoses readiness; Consulting structures the control roadmap; Starter implements one approved workflow; Growth runs ops + governed automation.

Safe / regulated AI workflow patterns

AI can help fintech ops — with guardrails:

  1. Prefer low-risk ops first — internal IT, carefully scoped support triage, non-production enrichment — before anything near money movement.
  2. Human-in-the-loop for decisions that affect customers, compliance outcomes, or ledger-adjacent systems.
  3. No “paste sensitive data into a public chatbot” patterns — out of scope.
  4. Logging, retention, and tool allowlists aligned to your policies.
  5. Agent design via autonomous AI agents; strategy via AI consulting.

Autonomy is earned: controls → pilot → production under Growth when the map says so.
