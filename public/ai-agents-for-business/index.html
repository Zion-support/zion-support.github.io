What is an AI agent, really?

An AI agent is software that doesn’t just answer a question — it does work. Given a goal, it plans steps, calls tools (search, CRM, database, code), checks its own output, and iterates until the task is done. Unlike a chatbot, it takes actions: rebooking a meeting, triaging a ticket, reconciling a billing line, patching a server.

For a business, the ROI is not “conversational AI.” It is removed calls, faster resolution, fewer errors, and staff who stop doing repetitive work.
