Model arena notes

A sheet for comparing models you already use. This page does not call a model, and it does not rank one for you.

Write one row per model

  • The model name and the task you will actually test.
  • The context length the task needs, in your own words.
  • The price unit you were quoted, and who quoted it.
  • What a wrong answer would cost, and who may approve a swap.
