What to include in your first message

A short overview is enough to start. These details help frame the conversation.

  • The task and the outcome

    Describe the issue or process, who it affects and what a useful result would look like.

  • The environment

    Name the relevant systems or equipment types. For a site visit, start with the city and country.

  • The timing

    Include your preferred dates, time zone and any maintenance-window constraints.

  • The starting point

    Mention what has already been tried, the main open question and who will review the next step.

Keep the first message focused on scope. Arrange access details and any sensitive technical material with the team through an appropriate channel.

From the first message to a clear next step

  1. Share the context

    Use the checklist to explain the need and the result you are seeking.

  2. Clarify the scope

    Identify missing information, responsibilities and the assumptions that need checking.

  3. Agree on the next step

    Confirm the scope and any proposal separately before scheduling or implementation.
