AI CONSULTING

Discovery of one operations process

A written map of one process — systems, owner, queue, and the call to build, wait, or do not automate. No client name and no invented payback percentage.

Situation

Operators arrive with “we need AI” and no named process, human owner, or kill rule. A board does not fund a twelve-month program without a cut. Zion’s published motion is the opposite: one process, an executive readout, a short session.

What we did

  • Map one real process: systems of record, queues, and where work stalls.
  • Write the cut for the decision-maker — what is already measured, who approves, what stays out of scope.
  • Call build, wait, or do not automate. If readiness fails, the result is stop — not a disguised funnel.
  • When readiness checks pass, consulting expands the map into a roadmap and effort order. An agent or automation comes only after that cut. Service detail: AI consulting.

Public outcome

A map a board can read. We do not publish the client, an internal queue, a confidential baseline, or a financial guarantee. We do not invent a certified case study or a savings percentage.
