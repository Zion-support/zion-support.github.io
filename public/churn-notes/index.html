Churn notes

The older Churn Radar page still shows broken styles. Use this note. It does not predict a score and it does not connect to a CRM.

Write down

  • The account name.
  • The signal a person already saw: a missed renewal, a quiet champion, an open ticket.
  • Who owns the next conversation.
  • The date of that conversation.


Bring the note to the person who owns the account. The page does not email them.
