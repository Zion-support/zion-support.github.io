Vendor notes

The older scorecard still links the same stuck directory as the other copilot pages. Write the answers you actually have. No score is computed.

Ask before you buy

An empty answer stays empty. Do not invent a rating.

  • What the vendor stores, and where.
  • Who can export or delete it, and how long that takes.
  • The notice the contract gives you before a price or a term changes.
  • The person at your company who accepts the risk.
