Pull request notes

A checklist a person fills in before a merge. Nothing on this page is cloned, scanned, or posted.

What to write down

  • What changed, in one sentence a reviewer can repeat.
  • Where a secret, a token, or a customer record would show up if someone pasted it.
  • Which tests a person ran, and which ones were skipped.
  • Who may merge, and where that decision is written.
