IT Field Services

Zion publishes onsite IT field-service pages for the countries listed below. Each live country page shows USD SKUs that are actually for sale on that page. We do not invent unpublished country catalogs or unofficial rates here.

SLAs on published pages are separated. Priority/SBD is for outages that cannot wait overnight. NBD is the workhorse tier for standard break/fix. 2BD IMAC is for planned change. Mixing these tiers incorrectly is a common way teams overspend.

T&M versus FTE answers a different question: effort or capacity. Convert from T&M to FTE once utilization stays high; convert back when the surge ends.

FAQ

Which country pages are published?

Brazil, Grenada, Haiti, Honduras, Hong Kong, and Hungary. Other locations start at Discovery $99 or Contact.

What is included in an incident price?

On published country pages, incident SKUs include transport plus the first hour onsite for that SLA when that SKU is listed. Additional hours use the matching Additional Hour SKU when present.

How do OOH and weekend uplifts work?

They multiply the base rate when work is outside business hours or on weekends/holidays. Percentages appear on each published country page and are confirmed before dispatch.

When should I buy T&M instead of an incident?

Use T&M when scope is open-ended (audits, migrations, unknown effort). Use incident SKUs when the ticket class and first-hour envelope are clear.

How does Discovery $99 fit before purchasing?

Discovery helps you decide if onsite field work is warranted versus remote fix or a packaged plan — including countries that are not yet published.
