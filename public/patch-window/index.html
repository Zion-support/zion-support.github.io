Use the Patch Window Estimator

Estimate elapsed time from system count, parallel capacity, per-system duration, rollback reserve and a safety buffer. Validate real dependencies, staffing, blackouts and customer approvals separately.

Method, assumptions and limits

Ceiling(system count ÷ parallel slots) × minutes per system + rollback reserve + safety buffer. Compare this with the available window.

Your values stay in this page's calculation code. This helper makes no network request and stores no inputs. Review assumptions with a qualified owner before taking action. It does not connect to your systems, use an AI backend, send an email or authorize paid work.
