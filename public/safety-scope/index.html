Safety scope

Scope comes before a pilot. Write what the agent may read, what it must not do, and who turns it off. This page records scope. It does not send text to a model.

Write down

  • One owner.
  • Data classes it may see.
  • Actions it must not take.
  • How you stop it today.


Take this note to the governance checklist. A review of evidence belongs on the review desk.
