Free Discovery guide

Choosing an AI or IT project should start with the problem, not a software purchase. Zion’s free online Discovery questionnaire gives you a structured way to describe the work you want to improve. It currently opens in Portuguese. Completing it does not require payment; any later implementation is an optional, separately scoped decision.

What to prepare

Describe your industry, the operational challenge, company size, desired timeline, existing use of AI, the outcome you care about and your deployment preference. Keep the first use case narrow. “Reduce the time spent classifying support requests” is more actionable than “automate the business.” Do not include credentials, personal customer records or confidential contracts.

For a useful starting brief, write down a baseline: approximately how often the task happens, who reviews its output and what a wrong result would cost. These are planning inputs, not independently verified business facts. A human should validate them before they appear in a business case.

What the current questionnaire provides

When you submit, the page displays rules-based app recommendations selected from your answers. These suggestions are a starting point for evaluation—not a completed systems audit, a custom AI model, or proof that the recommended app is integrated with your environment.

The questionnaire also requests email transmission of the submitted summary to your address and Zion’s Commercial team. An accepted provider request is not the same as arrival in both inboxes. If automatic submission cannot be confirmed, use the page’s manual-email fallback. Reliable dual-recipient delivery remains an acceptance requirement before Zion should promise delivery deadlines.

Explore the network without confusing pages with products

The Apps Network connects related catalog pages. Start from the hub to compare adjacent areas rather than assuming that every named route is a fully implemented application. Some routes currently contain placeholders or service explanations. Confirm whether a capability is an interactive tool, an informational page, a prototype or a separately scoped implementation before relying on it.

For cloud planning, inspect the Cloud Cost Estimator and document provider, region, usage and pricing assumptions. Estimates are not supplier quotes or savings guarantees. For broader operational questions, use the network hub to find relevant pages, then verify data handling and functional readiness.

Decide what happens next

A useful next step may be a small, explicitly scoped pilot—or a decision not to automate yet. Identify a measurable outcome, an owner, the information needed, approval requirements and a rollback path. Keep any optional paid work distinct from the free questionnaire. The discovery should remain accessible online without requiring a card or a purchase.
