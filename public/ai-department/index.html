The tier is priced per user per month for the platform and support, plus a retained head of AI. The exact number is set in Discovery from your seat count, workflow and compliance context — Zion does not publish an invented "from $X" or a catalog discount.

FAQ

Is there a free Discovery?

Yes. The AI/IT Discovery questionnaire at /discovery/ is online and free. It maps one process and returns an executive report; a specialist can follow up the same day.

Is the Zion AI Department a packaged SKU?

No. It is a productized engagement: defined components, a named owner and a monthly cadence, with scope and pricing set per company in Discovery.

Can we pay per outcome instead of a monthly retainer?

Yes, for the pilot. The per-outcome option is priced on tickets resolved, leads qualified or documents processed, and is agreed in writing before work starts.

What is the Zion AI App Network?

A set of interlinked, free-to-try apps for FinOps, operations, governance, field work and AI controls. The network is the platform layer the AI Department operates.

Where are you based and who do you serve?

Zion Tech Group is based in Middletown, Delaware, USA, and serves clients in the US, Brazil and other LatAm markets, with field operations and remote delivery.
