Carbon notes

What a person writes down about a page and its host. Nothing on this page estimates emissions or buys an offset.

What to write down

  • The page address, its weight, and how many times people open it in a month you already counted.
  • Who hosts it, and whether that host publishes an energy note.
  • The spend note if the hosting bill is the thing you can change.
  • A price for help sits on the published plans. This note does not add one.
