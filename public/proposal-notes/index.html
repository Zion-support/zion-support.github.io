Proposal notes

A proposal on this page is a written outline you fill in yourself. Nothing is generated, priced, exported, or sent.

What to write down

  • Who the note is for, and the job they asked about.
  • What is in scope, and what is left out.
  • A timeline written in weeks, not a start date this page can book.
  • Questions still open. Discovery is $99 and is not a proposal fee.
