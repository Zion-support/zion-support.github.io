Content notes

A draft brief you fill in yourself. Nothing is published, translated, or sent from this page.

What to write down

  • The reader, the page it will live on, and the person who can publish it.
  • The source you already have: a brief, a transcript, or a page on this site.
  • Words that must stay as written, and words a reviewer still has to approve.
  • Where the draft stops. A draft without an owner stays a draft.
