Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Zion Tech Group collects on ziontechgroup.com and during paid work, why we collect it, and how to ask for a copy or deletion. We do not sell personal data.

Effective date: 13 July 2026 · Last reviewed: 23 September 2026

1. Who we are

Zion Tech Group is an AI and IT services company. Controller contact:

  • Email: kleber@ziontechgroup.com
  • Phone: +1 302 464 0950
  • Mail: 364 E Main St STE 1008, Middletown, DE 19709, United States

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you send us or what is required to complete a purchase or meeting:

  • Contact details you type into forms or email — name, work email, phone, company, and the problem you describe.
  • Scheduling data when you book on Calendly (name, email, timezone, notes).
  • Payment data handled by Stripe on Stripe’s domain. We receive confirmation that a published plan was paid (Discovery $99, Consulting $499, Starter $2,500, Growth $8,000/month). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical logs that hosting and DNS providers keep (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to keep the site up and to investigate abuse.
  • Cookies needed to serve pages. See the Cookie Policy.

We do not scrape extra profiles, buy marketing lists for this site, or build a secret advertising identity from your visit.

3. How we use information

  • Reply to inquiries and run Discovery, consulting, or delivery work you asked for.
  • Send invoices, receipts, and contract documents.
  • Operate and secure the website (uptime, malware, spam, fraud).
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting duties.

We do not sell personal data. We do not rent it. We do not trade it for advertising.

4. Processors we use

These companies process data so we can run the business. Each has its own terms:

  • Stripe — checkout for the four published plans.
  • Calendly — booking a Discovery or consult call.
  • GitHub Pages / DNS / email hosts — publishing this static site and delivering email.

We share data with a processor only when it is required for that task, under their confidentiality and security terms.

5. Retention

Inquiry email is kept while the conversation is active and for a reasonable follow-up window. Contracts, invoices, and tax records are kept for the period required by U.S. and Delaware law. You can ask us to delete marketing or inquiry records that are not legally required.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures: TLS on the public site, least-privilege access to inboxes and Stripe, and no card data stored on this static site. No method is perfect. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and regulators as required.

7. Your choices

Email kleber@ziontechgroup.com to:

  • Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate details
  • Ask for deletion where the law allows
  • Object to or restrict a processing activity

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you may also have rights under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA. You can complain to your local supervisory authority. We will still try to resolve the request directly first.

8. International transfers

The company is based in Delaware, United States. If you contact us from another country, your message is processed in the United States. Processors (Stripe, Calendly, hosting) may also store data in the U.S. or other regions they disclose in their policies.

9. Children

This site is for business buyers. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. The dates at the top are the record. Material changes will be reflected here before they apply to new collection.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: kleber@ziontechgroup.com or the contact page.
