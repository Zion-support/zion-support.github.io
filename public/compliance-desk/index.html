Compliance desk

The older auditor address still links hostnames that do not open. Use this desk. It does not certify a system, and it does not send the form anywhere.

Write down

  • The system name and the person who owns it.
  • The data it reads, in plain words.
  • Where a person reviews the output before it reaches a customer.
  • The date of the next review.


A filled note is not an ISO certificate and not an EU AI Act filing. It is the list you take to Discovery or to your own counsel.
