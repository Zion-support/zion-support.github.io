AI readiness quiz

Five questions to write down before a discovery call. This page does not score a company, and it does not store an answer.

Write down

  1. Which workflow would change first if a model helped, and who owns that workflow today?
  2. Where does the source data live, and who is allowed to read it?
  3. What must a person still approve before anything is sent or changed?
  4. What would you stop doing if the answer were wrong?
  5. Who pays for the model calls, and what monthly cap is already agreed?
