Published benchmarks converge on five to seven emails over 30 to 45 days for shorter cycles, and eight to twelve over 60 to 90 days for enterprise motions.

FAQ

How many emails should a sequence have?

Published benchmarks converge on five to seven over 30 to 45 days for shorter cycles, and eight to twelve over 60 to 90 days for enterprise motions. We size it to your cycle.

When does a lead go to sales?

On a behaviour trigger — a pricing visit, a reply or a booked call — with the context attached, not on a fixed day.

What about deliverability?

Authenticated sending domains, a clean opted-in list and one-click unsubscribe are part of the build.

What do you measure?

Pipeline: replies per 100 leads entered, SQL conversion, meetings booked and influenced revenue. Opens are not the measure.
