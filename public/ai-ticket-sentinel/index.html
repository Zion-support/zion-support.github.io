Ticket queue notes

What a person writes on a ticket before it moves. Nothing on this page is classified, deduped, or escalated.

What to write down

  • The customer, the symptom, and the last time it worked.
  • Who owns the next reply, and by when.
  • What would make a person wake someone up.
  • Where the runbook says to look first.
