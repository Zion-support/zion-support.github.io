Audio cue sheet

This sheet is for a support or field call you already record. It does not synthesize a voice and it does not place a call.

Write down

  • The sentence the agent says before recording.
  • What is stored: audio, text, or both.
  • Who can open the file.


Do not imitate a customer's voice or a coworker's voice. Say that a recording is in progress.
