Edge notes

Write where the data is created before anyone talks about running a model next to it. Nothing here is deployed, scanned, or priced.

Write these down

  • Where the reading is created, and whether that reading can leave the building.
  • The delay you can live with, in a sentence a customer would understand.
  • What still works when the network link is down.
  • Who owns the device, and who is allowed to change the software on it.
