SLA notes

The older forecast and the older breach sentinel do not pick up fixes. One of them still links a host that does not open. Write the promise here. No uptime number is invented.

Write down

A note is not an incident, and it is not a credit.

  • The service, the promise, and the window.
  • How you would notice a miss, and who is allowed to say so.
  • The customer sentence, written before you need it.
  • Who may offer a credit, if the contract allows one.
