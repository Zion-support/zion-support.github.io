Use the Incident Cost Calculator

Combine revenue-at-risk assumptions with engineering and support labor to estimate incident exposure. The result is a planning scenario, not an accounting statement of lost revenue.

Method, assumptions and limits

Downtime hours × hourly revenue × affected percentage, plus engineering and support hours × their respective hourly costs.

Your values stay in this page's calculation code. This helper makes no network request and stores no inputs. Review assumptions with a qualified owner before taking action. It does not connect to your systems, use an AI backend, send an email or authorize paid work.
