Field route notes

Older field shells still point at country hosts, industry hosts, and a few pages that 404. Those addresses stay unpublished. Write the route here. Nothing on this page is dispatched, stocked, or priced.

Where the route is written

  • City names stay as anchors on the field city desk and the coverage roster.
  • The visit plan is the field plan desk. Parts stay on the parts desk. Coverage hours stay on the field coverage desk.

Industry pages that already exist

  • Energy and utilities, logistics, and manufacturing.
  • Public sector, retail, and construction.

What this page does not add

No new country page, no postal shell, and no museum shell. A hostname such as field-energy, field-banking, or field-logistics is not a separate site. If a link leaves this host, use the hub above instead.
