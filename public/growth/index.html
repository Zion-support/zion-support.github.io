Growth notes

A growth brief you fill in yourself. Nothing is launched, bid, or measured from this page.

What to write down

  • One audience and one offer. The campaign note holds the sentence a person must approve.
  • The channel you already run, and who is allowed to change the spend.
  • The page the button opens. Check that it loads before anyone treats a click as real.
  • A price for help sits on the published plans. This note does not add one.
