Caption brief

The older caption page sent people to hosts whose certificates do not match the name, and it listed inboxes that are not the contact for this site. Use this brief. Nothing is generated, uploaded, or scored.

Write down

  • Where the caption will appear, and who is supposed to read it.
  • The one claim, and the proof you can show for it.
  • The words you will not use, and who approves the line before it is posted.

A brief is not an engagement forecast and not a published caption.
