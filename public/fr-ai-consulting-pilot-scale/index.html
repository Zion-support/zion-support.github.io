AI is the Amplifier, Not the Starting Point

Based on the 2025–2026 research on AI consulting (McKinsey, Gartner, Highspot): AI should not be the starting point — it should be the amplifier. Value prop must anchor on:

  • What generalist AI can't do — human support, specialization, accountability
  • Measurable outcomes — ROI, efficiency gains, not just AI adoption
  • Pilot-to-scale methodology — phases: pilot → validate → scale
