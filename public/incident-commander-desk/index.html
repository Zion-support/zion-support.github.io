Incident commander desk

The older incident commander page still sends one link to a status hostname that does not open. Use this note instead. Nothing is paged, and nothing is uploaded.

What to name before the first update

  • One commander. A second person covers comms if the commander is in the bridge.
  • A severity the team already uses. Do not invent a new scale on this page.
  • The sentence a person will post, in words a customer can act on.
  • The time of the next update, even if the next update is "no change".
