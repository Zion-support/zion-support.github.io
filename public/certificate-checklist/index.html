Certificate checklist

Record the name people type, the name printed on the certificate, when it expires, and who renews it. This page stays in the browser. It does not open a connection to the host and it does not grade a cipher suite.

Name

The hostname in the address bar must match the name on the certificate, including the subdomain.

Date

Write the expiry as a calendar date and the person who receives the renewal reminder.

Owner

Name the team that can replace the certificate. If no one owns it, do not point a new app at that host.
