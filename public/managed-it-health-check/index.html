IT health check notes

A health check here is a list a person fills in. This page does not scan a network, open a port, or read a directory.

Write down

  • Who owns identity, backups, and the certificate list.
  • The systems that would stop the business if they stopped tonight.
  • The last restore that a person actually tried, and when.
  • What is still on a spreadsheet instead of an owner.
