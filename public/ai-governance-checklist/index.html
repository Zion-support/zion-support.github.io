Illustrative planning only. No observed saving, legal entitlement, contract interpretation or governance assurance is established. Check contract caps, tiers, exclusions and billing periods before using SLA figures. Never submit credentials or sensitive customer data.

Inputs remain in this page session. No upload, server storage, model inference, payment or email is performed. JavaScript is required for interactive results.

Method: manual cost = hours × hourly rate; annual cost = monthly cost × 12. Allowed downtime = period hours × (1 − uptime/100). Excess = max(0, actual − allowed). Hourly credit = period fee × credit%/100; total = excess × hourly credit. No contract cap or tier is applied.
