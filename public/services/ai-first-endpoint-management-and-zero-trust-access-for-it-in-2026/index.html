Endpoint management and zero trust access

This page is the guide the older stub was sending people to. It describes the work. It is not a product certificate and it does not claim a compliance seal.

What you bring

  • A device list: laptops, shared stations, and anything that reaches production systems.
  • The identity provider you already use, and who can approve a new device.
  • The two or three apps that must stay reachable during a rollout.

What Zion maps

Inventory before policy

Unknown devices are named before access rules change. A rule with no owner is a note, not a control.

Access by role

Each sensitive app gets a person who approves access, a review date, and a path for contractors that expires.

Rollout in one wave

The first wave is a single team. The readout says what broke, what stayed open, and what waits for the next wave.

What this is not

Zion does not publish a zero-trust badge on this page. Device encryption, MDM enrollment, and conditional access are checked against your tenant during the engagement, not assumed here.

Start with Discovery $99 if the device list is still a spreadsheet. If the scope is already one team, see Plans and managed IT.
