What breaks after go-live

Silent tool failures

The CRM field moved. The ITSM queue renamed. The agent still “succeeds” and writes garbage. Maintenance watches outcomes, not just HTTP 200.

Prompt and routing rot

A workaround from week two becomes the default path. Weekly tuning is cheaper than a quarterly incident.

Spend surprises

Retry storms and unscoped retrieval show up as invoices. We treat retries as a cost event. Deeper waste maps stay on FinOps.

No one on the kill switch

The original owner left. Maintenance names a new one or Zion holds the pause until you do.
