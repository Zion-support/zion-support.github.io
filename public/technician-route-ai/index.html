Route notes

The stops for one day, in the order a person chose. No traffic feed is read, and no route is sent to a driver.

Write down

  • The start point and the last stop.
  • Each stop, the task, and the window.
  • Which stop needs a part picked up first.
  • Who may drop a stop if the day runs long.
