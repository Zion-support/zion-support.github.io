Security control notes

A control list. Nothing is scanned, blocked, or deployed from this page.

What to write down

  • The system, and the person who can turn a control on.
  • Which actions need a second person: a send, a delete, a new admin.
  • Where secrets live, and who rotates them. Do not paste a secret here.
  • How you turn the control off. This page does not change a firewall.
