Chatbot notes

A bot brief you fill in yourself. Nothing is deployed, trained, or connected from this page.

What to write down

  • The corpus you already own, and the person who can change an article.
  • Which questions the bot may answer, and which ones go to a person.
  • How a reader opens the citation. An answer without a source stays in draft.
  • What counts as contained, and what still needs a person. Use the deflection desk for the numbers you already have.
