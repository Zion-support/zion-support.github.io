Incident response notes

Name the owner, the severity word you will actually use, and the sentence a person posts. This page does not page anyone and does not show an uptime number.

Before an incident

  • The person who decides severity, and the backup if they are offline.
  • The three words you will use for severity, written down in advance.
  • Where the timeline is written, in a system you already have.
  • Who speaks to customers, and who does not.

The sentence

One sentence: what people can and cannot do right now. Add the next update time. Leave the cause out until someone has checked it.
