Data quality notes

A checklist you fill in yourself. Nothing is scanned, scored, or connected from this page.

What to write down

  • The table or feed, and the person who can change it.
  • Freshness you already expect, in the units you already use.
  • Which fields a downstream report is allowed to trust.
  • What a person does when a row looks wrong. This page does not open a ticket.
