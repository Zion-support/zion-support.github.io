Transcript desk

Use this before you record a meeting. The note stays in the browser. It does not upload audio.

Write down

  • Who agreed to be recorded.
  • The speakers you expect.
  • How long the notes are kept, and who deletes them.


If consent is blank, do not record. Meeting notes without audio are on the notes page.
