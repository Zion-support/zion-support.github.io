An AI operations retainer keeps production AI running

Most AI programs do not fail at the pilot. They fail after launch, when models drift, costs creep, prompts need review and incidents need someone accountable. An AI operations retainer gives you a defined squad responsible for running AI in production: monitoring, evaluation, FinOps, incident response and continuous improvement, with clear work packages and measurable outcomes instead of open-ended hours.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Zion Tech Group delivery based?

Zion delivers from Brazil with US time-zone overlap. Engineering, management and commercial communication run in English, with Portuguese and Spanish available for regional work.

How much time-zone overlap do we get with US teams?

Typically 4–8 working hours with US Eastern and 6–8 with US Central, which is enough for same-day reviews, standups and incident response.

How do you protect intellectual property and data?

Work is contracted with IP assignment, access is least-privilege, and we support data-residency options, approval gates and audit trails for regulated workloads.

Do you work with our in-house team or replace it?

Usually both. We embed a defined squad alongside your team, with named leads and clear ownership, rather than a black-box offshore bench.

What kinds of work fit nearshore delivery best?

AI pilots and agent operations, cloud and DevOps, application modernization, data engineering, and managed AI operations with governance.

How do we start?

Start with a free Discovery: one process, one report, one 30-minute session. From there we scope a bounded pilot with measurable KPIs.

Is this a staff augmentation contract?

No. A retainer is scoped around outcomes and work packages with a named squad, not hours of generic staffing.

How is scope decided each month?

Through bounded work packages with acceptance criteria, reviewed against the KPI baseline set during Discovery and the pilot.

Can we start with a pilot instead of a retainer?

Yes. Most engagements start with a free Discovery and a bounded pilot, then convert to a retainer once the operating model is proven.
