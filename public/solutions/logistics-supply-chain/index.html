Logistics and supply chain

Dispatch, spare parts, and depot stock, scoped to one lane or one warehouse.

What a first engagement covers

  • Name the dispatch or parts process that misses its commitment.
  • Separate a routing pilot from a fleet replacement.
  • Track return status without standing up a new system of record.
  • Decide whether the bottleneck is data, labor, or the route.

How the first week is scoped

Pick one lane, one depot, or one parts cage. The first week writes who assigns the work, what the driver or clerk already sees, and which number the supervisor actually uses. A routing sketch is not a new transportation system.

What to bring to Discovery

  • The lane or warehouse, and the commitment it misses.
  • The system that assigns work today.
  • Whether the miss is a missing part, a late truck, or a bad address.
