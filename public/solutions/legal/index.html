Legal operations

Contract reading and obligation tracking for a team that already has counsel. This page is not legal advice, and it does not file anything.

What a first engagement covers

  • Map one review: a contract, a renewal date, or an obligation list.
  • Keep counsel in the loop on any sentence that leaves the building.
  • Separate a reading aid from a legal conclusion.
  • Use the governance checklist before a model drafts a customer letter.

How the first week is scoped

Name the document, the person who may sign it, and the date someone already tracks. A summary on this site is not a legal opinion. The first week stops when that handoff is written down.

What to bring to Discovery

  • One contract type or one obligation list.
  • Who is allowed to change the language.
  • Where the dates live today, even if that is a spreadsheet.
