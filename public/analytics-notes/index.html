Analytics notes

A reading list for numbers you already have. Nothing is connected, alerted, or exported from this page.

What to write down

  • The report name, and the person who can explain a change.
  • The grain: a day, an account, or a ticket queue.
  • Which number would make you call someone, and who that is.
  • Where the source table lives. This page does not open a warehouse.
