Pipeline notes

A brief for a pipeline you already run. Nothing is built, estimated, or connected from this page.

What to write down

  • The source, the destination, and the person who can change either.
  • How late a load can be before someone looks.
  • The contract fields a consumer is allowed to trust.
  • What you do when a load fails. This page does not retry it.
