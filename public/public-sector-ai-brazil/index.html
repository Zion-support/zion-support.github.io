FAQ

Do you work with government agencies or as a supplier?

Both. We help agencies modernize and secure services, and we help suppliers prepare evidence-ready bids for public notices.

Which procurement rules apply?

Federal, state and municipal procurement in Brazil is governed by Lei 14.133 and executed through portals such as Compras.gov.br. We scope each engagement to the applicable notice and modality.

Can you help us respond to an edital?

Yes. Edital Desk and RFP Readiness turn a notice into a brief and an evidence score, so your response is complete before submission.

How do you handle data protection and security?

With approval gates, audit trails, least-privilege access and, where required, data-residency options and private model environments.

Do you publish fixed prices?

No. Public and regulated work is scoped after a free Discovery. Taxes, fees and operational costs are stated separately.
