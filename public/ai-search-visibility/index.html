What moves AI visibility

Answer first

The direct answer in the opening lines, not after a page of preamble. AI engines extract from the top of the page.

Named numbers and sources

Real figures with a named source, replacing adjectives. Original data is the most durable citation.

Question-shaped headings

Headings written as the questions buyers actually ask, each with a self-contained answer.

Ungated content

The best thinking stays readable. Content behind a form cannot be quoted by anyone.

Current pages

The pages that matter stay accurate and recently reviewed.

Structured data and crawlability

Schema markup, clean HTML and a robots.txt that does not block AI crawlers.

Off-site mentions

Trade publications, review sites, partner pages and communities where buyers ask each other.

FAQ

Is GEO different from SEO?

SEO optimises for ranking in a list of links. GEO optimises for being mentioned, cited or recommended inside an answer. Both matter; the mechanics differ.

How do you measure it?

By running a fixed set of buyer questions through the engines and recording mention rate, citation rate and recommendation rate against named competitors.

Will you guarantee a top position?

No. No one can guarantee how an AI engine answers. We guarantee the work: answer-first pages, named sources, schema, and a monthly review.

Do we need to pay for ads?

No. Paid channels are separate. GEO works on owned content and earned mentions.

How fast do results appear?

Usually weeks to months. The engines refresh on their own schedule, so we review monthly and keep the pages current.
