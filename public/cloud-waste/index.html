Use the Cloud Waste Scenario Estimator

Estimate a possible monthly opportunity from your own compute and storage spend and assumed unused percentages. This helper does not inspect cloud accounts, detect idle resources or guarantee savings.

Method, assumptions and limits

Compute spend × idle percentage + storage spend × unused percentage. Remaining spend is the input baseline minus that scenario opportunity.

Your values stay in this page's calculation code. This helper makes no network request and stores no inputs. Review assumptions with a qualified owner before taking action. It does not connect to your systems, use an AI backend, send an email or authorize paid work.
