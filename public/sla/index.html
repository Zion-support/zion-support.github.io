Service level

We do not invent a public uptime number or a one-size credit table. If an engagement needs an SLA, it is written in the Growth or managed-IT contract after the work is scoped.

Discovery ($99) and Consulting ($499) are diagnostic products. They do not include a production SLA. Starter ($2,500) is a first production outcome plus 30 days of support — the written order names the system. Growth ($8,000/month) is the retainer path where response windows, escalation, and credits can be defined.
