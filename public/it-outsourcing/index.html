IT outsourcing notes

A staffing note you fill in yourself. No rate is quoted, and no ticket is assigned from this page.

What to write down

  • The work that stays with your team, and the work a vendor would run.
  • Who owns the ticket when something fails, including after hours.
  • The systems the vendor may touch, and the ones they may not.
  • Read the comparison note before you treat a price as real. This page does not publish one.
