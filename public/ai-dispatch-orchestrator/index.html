Dispatch orchestrator notes

Who gets the next job, written down before anyone is assigned. Nothing on this page is routed or paged.

What to write down

  • The queue name and the hours a person watches it.
  • The fallback person when the first owner is out.
  • What urgent means in writing, before anyone is paged.
  • The window the dispatch note already lists.
