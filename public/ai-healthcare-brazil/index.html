AI in healthcare: support, with a physician in the loop

CFM Resolution 2.454/2026 treats AI as a support tool for medical practice: it requires human supervision, guarantees the patient's right to information and refusal, and obliges institutions that develop or use AI to run a preliminary risk assessment and, where applicable, maintain an AI and Telemedicine Committee. Zion builds and operates that layer — governance, data protection and monitoring — and does not sell a packaged clinical product.

FAQ

Does Zion sell a clinical or diagnostic product?

No. We build the operational, governance and data-protection layer. Clinical and diagnostic decisions belong to the physician.

What does CFM 2.454/2026 require?

A preliminary risk assessment, human supervision of AI-supported decisions, patient information and, where applicable, an AI and Telemedicine Committee.

When did it take effect?

180 days after publication; the resolution was published on 27 February 2026.

How do you handle health data?

Under the LGPD, with privacy by design, security measures sized to the data and contractual clauses.
