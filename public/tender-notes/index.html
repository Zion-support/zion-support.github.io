Tender notes

The older AI Tender Scout page still shows broken styles. Use this note. It does not search a portal and it does not submit a bid.

Write down

  • The notice name and the deadline.
  • The person who decides go or no-go.
  • The evidence you still do not have.
  • Leave price blank. Do not invent a bid amount.


A filled note is the list you take into Discovery. It is not a proposal.
