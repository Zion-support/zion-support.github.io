Use the RFP Evidence Readiness Checklist

Run a ten-part evidence self-check and identify the areas still needing confirmation. The score measures checklist completeness only, not legal compliance, buyer evaluation or likelihood of winning.

Method, assumptions and limits

Ten confirmed evidence areas, worth 10 points each. Leave an item unchecked if it is missing, unknown or not yet reviewed. Document buyer-specific weighting separately.
