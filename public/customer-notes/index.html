Customer notes

Older pages link a customer record, a feedback inbox, and a CRM. None of those open from here. Write the note a person will say out loud.

Write down

  • The account, the person you will speak with, and the date.
  • The last promise that was made, and whether it was kept.
  • The question for this call. One question.

This is not a health score, not a forecast, and not a message that gets sent.
