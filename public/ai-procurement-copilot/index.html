Procurement notes

A sourcing note you fill in yourself. Nothing is purchased, scored, shortlisted, or sent.

What to write down

  • What you need to buy, and what is out of scope.
  • Vendors already known to the team. This page does not look them up.
  • The date a person will decide, written by that person.
  • Questions still open. Discovery is $99 and is not a sourcing fee.
