AI & automation · Practical guide

Choose the right AI app for your workflow

A useful evaluation begins with one real task: triaging a support request, preparing a sales handoff, or finding an answer in approved documents. Write down what happens today before choosing a tool. That gives you a fair comparison and makes the next step easier to scope.

In this guide
  1. 1. Define the task and its boundaries
  2. 2. Compare the complete workflow
  3. 3. Test with a clear acceptance rule

1. Define the task and its boundaries

Describe the input, the expected output, and the person responsible for accepting it. Separate assistance, such as drafting a response, from an action, such as sending it. Decide which steps always need a person to review them.

  • Name the team and the recurring task.
  • Collect a small set of representative examples, including difficult cases.
  • Record current turnaround time and the common reasons for rework.

2. Compare the complete workflow

Check what the app actually does today. A product description, an interactive demonstration, and an integration with your own systems are different stages. Ask how data enters the workflow, where results appear, and how errors reach a person.

  • Identify required connectors and permissions.
  • Check whether a result can be edited, exported, or traced to its source.
  • Ask what happens when information is missing or the service is unavailable.
  • Confirm the scope and price for your use case before committing.

3. Test with a clear acceptance rule

Use the same examples for each candidate and have the people doing the work review the outputs. Measure accepted results and time spent correcting them, rather than counting generated responses. Use approved sample data and agree how it will be handled before connecting production systems.

  • Record useful output, incorrect output, and cases requiring escalation.
  • Include human review and integration effort in the cost comparison.
  • Choose a narrow pilot with an owner and a decision date.
