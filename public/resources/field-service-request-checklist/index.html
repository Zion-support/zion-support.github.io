Field services · Practical guide

Prepare a field service request that can be scoped

A clear brief helps the service team assess the work, identify the right skills, and ask fewer follow-up questions. Use this checklist for a site survey, equipment intervention, installation, or smart-hands request. Share sensitive access details only through an agreed secure channel.

In this guide
  1. 1. Describe the site and the work
  2. 2. Confirm access and the service window
  3. 3. Agree acceptance and closeout evidence

1. Describe the site and the work

Start with the location and the outcome you need. Distinguish diagnosis from a known repair, and state whether remote engineering support will direct the technician.

  • City, country, site type, and an operational contact.
  • Equipment manufacturer, model, quantity, and relevant fault symptoms.
  • Requested tasks, exclusions, and any required skills or certifications.
  • Available spares, tools, cabling, and responsibility for supplying them.

2. Confirm access and the service window

Specify a date, time, and time zone. Explain access procedures early so that security checks or site restrictions can be considered when availability is reviewed.

  • Preferred service window and any outage or change restrictions.
  • Check-in instructions, escort requirements, and authorization contact.
  • Remote bridge details and escalation path, when applicable.
  • Travel, freight, parking, or other site-specific costs to include in the quote.

3. Agree acceptance and closeout evidence

Define the evidence needed to close the task before work starts: test results, a service report, approved photographs, or an inventory update. Keep credentials and unnecessary personal data out of routine reports.

  • Name the person who will accept completion.
  • Specify the tests and required service-report fields.
  • Clarify what to do if the fault, access conditions, or scope changes.
  • Review the written scope and price. Scheduling follows formal client confirmation, a received purchase order, and Zion operational release.

Your next step
