Workforce notes

A workforce review on this page is a list you write yourself. Nothing is scored, ranked, uploaded, or sent.

What to write down

  • The team and the question: hiring, load, or a role that is open.
  • Facts the company already keeps, such as headcount and open roles. This page does not pull them in.
  • What a person will read, and who signs off before anyone acts on it.
  • Questions still open. Discovery is $99 and is not a workforce study.
