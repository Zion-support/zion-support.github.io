Roadmap for Agentic Implementation

Based on proven agent-lifecycle methodologies (embedding → capabilities building → orchestration), the Zion AI Consulting Roadmap is a phased approach that reduces risk at every stage.

Phase 1: Strategy & Planning (Weeks 1–4)

Discovery & Assessment

Identify high-value workflows, map data flows, and quantify the business case for automation.

Use Case Selection

Select a pilot use case with clear business value, manageable complexity, and a named business-owner sponsor.

Baseline Establishment

Measure current-state performance (cost, time, quality) to create a baseline for ROI measurement.

Governance Setup

Define governance guardrails: human-in-the-loop thresholds, compliance requirements, and escalation rules.

Phase 2: Pilot & Validation (Weeks 5–12)

Agent Build & Integration

Build the pilot agent, connect to your tools, and implement the core functionality.

Validation & Testing

Validate against the baseline, measure performance, and document successes and limitations.

Customer Feedback Loop

Gather feedback from internal stakeholders and adjust the agent before scaling.

Internal Advocacy

Identify internal champions who will advocate for the pilot and support scaling.

Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Weeks 13–26)

Production Deployment

Deploy the validated agent to production with monitoring, alerting, and human-in-the-loop fallback.

Iterative Improvement

Collect feedback, identify optimization opportunities, and improve the agent over time.

Expansion & Roll-Out

Expand to additional use cases, teams, and geographies.

Continuous Optimization

Use AI-driven analytics to continuously improve agent performance, cost, and business outcomes.
