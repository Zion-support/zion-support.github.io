LLM bench notes

The benchmark explainer still links this address. Write the task, the inputs you are allowed to use, and how you will tell a pass from a fail. This page does not run a model.

A bench you can repeat

  • The task, with one example of a good answer and one of a bad answer.
  • Where the inputs came from, and whether a customer row is in them. If it is, stop.
  • Who scores the result, and the date they last did it.
  • What you will change if the score moves, and what you will leave alone.
