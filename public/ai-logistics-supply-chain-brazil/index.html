FAQ

Do you sell a TMS or a routing product?

No. We build the data and decision layer around the systems you already run, and integrate with your ERP/TMS.

What comes first, forecasting or routing?

Whichever decision has the best data and the highest cost of being wrong. The discovery sets that order.

How do you handle uncertainty?

Every forecast ships with a stated horizon and confidence, and exceptions route to a person.

How is value measured?

Against a written baseline for the pilot — cost per delivery, service level or inventory — never against a promise.
