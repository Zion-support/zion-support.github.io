1. Capacity and cost

Estimate the time spent today, then compare a cloud-cost scenario. Keep assumptions separate from measured savings.

  • ROI Calculator — time and value scenarios · Source
  • FinOps Estimator — cost planning · Source

Test: document inputs, record the current monthly baseline and compare the same workload after a reversible trial. A calculator result is not a quote or promised saving.

2. Governance and reliability

Review permissions and human oversight before connecting reliability targets to operational commitments.

  • AI Governance Checklist — controls review · Source
  • SLA Calculator — availability scenarios · Source

Test: name an owner, list evidence gaps and define an escalation threshold. Scores do not certify compliance; uptime calculations do not establish a contractual SLA.

3. Communications

Explore routing and meeting-review concepts, then check what the actual implementation supports.

  • Smart Call Routing AI · Source
  • Meeting Intelligence AI · Source

Test: use a synthetic example and a written review rubric. Do not upload real call recordings or personal data until consent, permissions and retention are approved. A landing page does not prove live AI inference.

A repeatable experiment

  1. Write the problem and name the process owner.
  2. Measure a baseline and choose one outcome.
  3. Inspect the source and distinguish functional tools from prototypes or concepts.
  4. Use synthetic or explicitly approved data.
  5. Set human review, a stop rule and a review date.
  6. Record what changed before deciding whether to expand.
