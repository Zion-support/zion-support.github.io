What the Free Discovery gives you

The Discovery is online and free. It produces an immediate on-screen report with matched apps and an indicative capacity estimate based on the answers supplied. The form attempts simultaneous email delivery to the client and commercial@ziontechgroup.com; browser copy/download and email fallback protect the result if automatic delivery is unavailable.

Continue from the report to a responsible pilot

Check the assumptions, name an owner and define a human review gate, a success metric and a stop rule before implementation. A report is a planning hypothesis, not a guarantee of savings or authorization to change production. Email-provider acceptance does not prove receipt in both inboxes. Keep a copy of your report and verify the recipients.
