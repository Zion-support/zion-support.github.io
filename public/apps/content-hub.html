Operations & reliability: start with evidence

Use this practical checklist to turn an IT incident, change window or field-service need into a clear technical brief. These are planning prompts, not automated incident handling or a guarantee of recovery.

Continue with a relevant resource

  1. Define the affected service, business impact and first observed time.
  2. Collect system identifiers, relevant logs, recent changes and approved access windows.
  3. Record the requested outcome, owner, constraints and agreed verification method.

Evidence to prepare

  • Affected asset or service and location, with no credentials in the brief
  • Incident timeline, symptoms and reproducible observations
  • Monitoring readings and SLA or service-window context
  • Dependencies, relevant changes and rollback considerations
  • Named requester, contact route and priority rationale

Before any physical action

A technical brief is not dispatch authorization. Before purchase, supplier reservation, mobilization or dispatch, Zion requires written client confirmation, the corresponding purchase order (PO), and separate Zion operational release.
