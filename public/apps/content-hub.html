Zion Apps Content Hub

Every free app, batch page and spotlight in the Zion Apps Network in one place — grouped by job to be done, all interlinked. Not sure where to start? The Free Discovery recommends the right apps for your process in 5 minutes and emails the results to you instantly.

🆕 New this week (Oct 4, 2026)

  • LLM Cost Calculator — Estimate monthly and yearly LLM API spend from request volume, tokens per request and your provider's per-token prices. Runs 100% in your browser.
  • Token Cost Estimator — Paste a prompt or document and get an approximate token count plus the cost per call at the price you enter. Heuristic estimate (about 4 characters per token for English), computed locally.
  • Kubernetes Manifest Linter — Paste a Kubernetes YAML manifest and get instant best-practice findings: missing resource limits, :latest images, missing probes, privileged containers, host networking and more. Nothing leaves your browser.
  • AI Ticket Triage — Paste a ticket and get a suggested category, priority (P1–P4) and routing queue in one click. Transparent keyword rules you can copy into your ITSM — runs locally.
  • Support Ticket Triager (bulk) — Paste a backlog — one ticket per line — and get a sortable triage table with category, priority and queue for every ticket. Export as CSV for your helpdesk.
  • Maintenance Ticket Triage — For facilities and field maintenance: pick the asset, impact and symptom to get a severity, dispatch type (remote vs on-site) and response target based on the SLA hours you define.
  • AI Ticket Sentinel — SLA breach watch — Paste open tickets (id, priority, opened time) and see which ones are breached or at risk against your SLA hours. A simple early-warning board for service desks and MSPs.
  • AI Meeting Notes Copilot — Paste raw meeting notes or a transcript and get action items, decisions, open questions and owners extracted into a clean recap you can paste into email or Notion.
  • Site Uptime Watchdog — Enter a list of URLs and check from your browser whether each one responds and how long it takes. Re-check on an interval while the tab is open. A quick sanity check before you set up full monitoring.
  • Partner Referral Tracker — Track partner referrals — partner, client, stage and deal value — with totals per partner and CSV export. Data is saved only in your browser (localStorage).
  • AI Data Warehouse Sizer — Size a data warehouse before you buy: rows per day, bytes per row, retention and compression give you raw and compressed storage, plus a readiness checklist for AI and analytics workloads.

🧰 Guias & ferramentas práticas (PT-BR)

  • Guia do comprador de Field Services & Smart Hands — Checklist prático e gerador de brief de RFP para contratar técnicos de campo e smart hands: escopo, janelas, SLA de chegada, acesso e evidência de conclusão.
  • Linha de base de custos de TI & FinOps — Planilha interativa grátis para montar a linha de base de custos de TI: cloud, SaaS, licenças, telecom e contratos, com alertas de renovação, dono ausente e baixo uso. Exporta CSV.
  • Priorizador de casos de uso de IA — Ferramenta grátis para pontuar e ranquear casos de uso de IA por valor, frequência, dados disponíveis, risco e esforço — com pesos ajustáveis e classificação em quick wins, estratégicos, testar depois e evitar.
  • Gerador de comunicação de incidentes — Gere em segundos as mensagens de incidente — abertura, atualização e resolução — com severidade, impacto, workaround e horário da próxima atualização. Grátis, no navegador.
