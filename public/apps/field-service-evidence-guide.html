Discovery stays free and online

From free Discovery to a field visit you can qualify

A planning page is not a confirmed technician, quote, purchase order or permission to dispatch. Validate every assumption with an accountable human owner.

Five evidence-led steps

  1. Choose one process in free online Discovery; retain and review its immediate report.
  2. Document authorized site details, local time zone, exact visit window, task, ticket, access requirements and acceptance criteria. A shared address does not imply a single visit.
  3. Verify named technicians, written availability, skills, tools, insurance and other compliance evidence before selection.
  4. Compare all-in supplier prices, travel, taxes, minimum hours and cancellation terms. Require written client approval and applicable PO before booking, ordering or dispatch.
  5. Record work completed, timestamps, tests, exceptions, client acceptance and actual costs without inventing results.

Before selecting a partner

  • Compare identical scopes and qualifications; do not infer stock or live coverage.
  • Keep assumptions labelled as estimates until evidence confirms them.
  • Use one reply thread and owner; avoid duplicate unanswered follow-ups.
  • Share customer and site data only with authorized recipients, minimally.
  • Never book, purchase or dispatch from a planning guide.
