Before you begin

Do I have to purchase consulting?

No. The Discovery report and self-service app journey are free. Paid implementation is a separate, optional choice after you review evidence.

Does the report prove that an app is production-ready?

No. Recommendations and catalog membership are starting points. Validate functionality, data handling, suitability and operational controls yourself before use.

Does a pilot plan authorize dispatch or purchases?

No. A planning document creates no service order. Zion requires formal client confirmation and a PO before site dispatch or purchases.

How can I continue with my team?

Save the report, name an owner, refine assumptions in the Report Workbench and decide the next gate in the Decision Kit.
