Security and compliance

Checklists and notes for security work. This page does not scan a network, send a phishing test, or score a vendor. Hosts that used to sit on compliance and governance names do not open.

Desks that open

  • Cybersecurity desk — Certificates, owners, and what a tool can reach.
  • Cybersecurity platform — The platform page on this site.
  • Compliance desk — The questions. Not a certificate.
  • Governance checklist — What a person will answer before a launch.
  • Certificate checklist — Names and expiry dates you already know.
  • Secret notes — Where a secret lives. Nothing is pasted into a scanner.
  • Accessibility checker — A browser check of markup you paste. No upload.
  • Review desk — Scope, evidence, and stop rules. No attack text.
  • Prompt shield desk — What the system may repeat. No jailbreak list.
  • Policy notes — An outline. Not legal advice.
  • Vendor notes — Questions before a purchase. No score.
  • Compliance scanner — The scanner page that stays on this domain.
  • SLA risk forecaster — The forecaster page on this site.
  • Recovery notes — What you would restore. No backup system is contacted.
