Operating decisions

1. Value before automation

Record baseline hours, costs and the expected benefit. Treat estimates as assumptions, not guaranteed savings.

2. Governance and supplier risk

Define a human approver, minimum access, data boundaries, an audit trail and a stop rule.

3. Service desk readiness

Check queue ownership, ticket quality and SLA evidence before adding automation.

4. Incident readiness

Write a runbook, impact criteria, rollback plan and evidence required for closure.

5. Onsite or remote?

Try safe remote diagnosis first. If onsite work is needed, review the companion field-services scope.

6. Procurement controls

Verify compatibility, complete costs, terms and formal authorization before buying or dispatching.

7. One reversible pilot

Choose one owner, a baseline, success metric, review date and stop rule. Separate a free experiment from optional paid implementation.
