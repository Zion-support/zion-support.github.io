Build one reversible 30-day pilot

  1. Days 1–5: name an owner, define the process boundary and measure the baseline.
  2. Days 6–10: use synthetic or properly authorized data; check permissions and compare a manual control.
  3. Days 11–20: run a limited test with human review, logs and a rollback path.
  4. Days 21–25: compare quality, time and exceptions against the baseline.
  5. Days 26–30: stop, revise or expand only after documented review and separate approval.

A readiness score is a hypothesis, not an audit, certified model or guaranteed saving. App maturity varies: inspect each page and repository. No purchase, appointment, dispatch or paid implementation is authorized by Discovery.
