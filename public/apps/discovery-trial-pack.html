A five-step pilot gate

  1. Owner: name one process owner and identify who can approve data access.
  2. Baseline: measure current volume, elapsed time, error rate and manual review effort.
  3. Bounded sample: use fictional or anonymized cases with an expected outcome; no passwords, payment details or confidential client records.
  4. Human review: agree who checks outputs, what must be escalated and a stop rule for unsafe or incorrect responses.
  5. Decision: document build, wait or do-not-build. A trial is not approval to buy, dispatch, integrate or put an agent into production.
