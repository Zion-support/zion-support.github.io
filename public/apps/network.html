Keep a useful record of your test

  1. State the question, owner and measured starting point. Choose one success measure before testing.
  2. Use synthetic or explicitly authorized data. Record assumptions, permissions and anything the resource cannot yet do.
  3. Save the observed result, exceptions and reviewer decision. Expand only after comparing the result with the starting point.

Understand the resource you open

  • A local tool calculates or organizes information in your browser; it does not establish a connection to your business systems.
  • A guide or demonstration helps you explore a process; a sample result is not a verified operational result.
  • A repository provides source to inspect. Check its implementation, license and deployment separately before relying on it.

Start with free Discovery

Discovery requires no card, purchase or mandatory meeting. The report appears in your browser, with copy and download options. The service requests email delivery to the validated participant and commercial@ziontechgroup.com; provider acceptance does not confirm inbox receipt. Use the manual option when needed. Paid implementation is optional and separate.
