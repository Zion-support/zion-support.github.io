No purchases, reservations, dispatch, uploads or emails are performed by this guide.

Before any purchase, reservation or dispatch, obtain formal written client confirmation of the exact scope and price, the corresponding purchase order (PO), and a separate, specific Zion operational release. All three requirements are mandatory.

Operating decisions

1. Site and access

Record the exact site, time window, onsite contact, escort/security requirements and parking or loading constraints.

2. Technical task and rollback

Identify devices, circuits, symptoms, tools and spares; write the permitted action and rollback boundary.

3. Acceptance evidence

Agree ticket reference, authorized photos/logs, tests and the proof needed before checkout. Never publish client credentials or confidential site details.

4. Supplier and commercial gate

Confirm named capability, written availability, complete costs, travel, taxes and terms.

5. Review before release

Check remote alternatives, scope completeness, acceptance tests, compatibility and authorization. Missing evidence means hold, not automatic scheduling.

Plan a multi-site switch replacement

For each site, list the number of devices, the proposed window and who can accept the work. Request a separate price and completion record for each phase.

  1. Site survey. Record rack space, power, cabling and access constraints.
  2. Staging. Agree on the configuration owner, asset labels and preparation checks.
  3. Storage and transport. Assign custody, insurance and delivery tracking through handover.
  4. Installation and activation. Define the approved change, remote support, tests and sign-off.
  5. Return of retired equipment. Record serial numbers, packaging, the authorized destination and proof of receipt.

Ask for weekday and out-of-hours rates, travel, taxes and volume discounts separately. This lets you compare like-for-like offers before you approve a visit.
