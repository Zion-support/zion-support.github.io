Find a practical path from IT problem to pilot

Choose a real operational problem first. Review relevant tools, gather evidence and involve an engineer when scope, onsite coverage or risk needs validation. The paths below are informational, not confirmed services, rates or outcomes.

1. Service desk and IT operations

Clarify ticket intake, incident escalation and operational handoffs before automation.

  • Managed IT Dashboard
  • SLA Calculator
  • Ops Runbook AI

Pilot measure: classification completeness or time to triage.

2. AI automation and agents

Define repetitive work, human approvals, access controls and failure handling.

  • AI Automation Platform
  • Agent Brief
  • AI Governance Checklist

Pilot measure: time saved and manual exceptions per case.

3. Costs and ROI

Document the current baseline, labor assumptions and dependencies before interpreting an estimate.

  • FinOps Estimator
  • ROI Calculator
  • Stack Audit

Pilot measure: measured cost change against a defined baseline.

4. Field engineers and smart hands

Capture site location, equipment, access, required time window, parts and support requirements before asking for a dispatch.

  • Field Services
  • SLA Calculator
  • Contact Zion

Pilot measure: complete and validated scope at first review.

Information that helps Zion qualify the request

  • Country, city, site and onsite access rules
  • Equipment make, model, symptoms and expected work
  • Desired service window, SLA and parts dependencies
  • Requester, approval owner and a measurable success criterion

Field-engineer availability, commercial terms and any dispatch remain subject to location and scope verification, formal customer confirmation, a validated purchase order and Zion's operational release.
