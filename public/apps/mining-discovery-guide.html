Your next five steps

  1. Complete free Discovery with one process, team hours and goal.
  2. Name a process owner and establish a measured baseline.
  3. Confirm data permissions; use non-sensitive, representative test data.
  4. Run a reversible, offline pilot with human review and explicit stop rules.
  5. Review error rates, uncertainty and operational impact before considering deployment.

Do not use these concepts for emergency response, safety approvals, regulatory certification, dam stability decisions or autonomous equipment control.
