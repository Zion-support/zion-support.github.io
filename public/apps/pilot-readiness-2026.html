From a tool result to a reviewable AI/IT pilot

Start with a business question, not a software promise. Use the public planning tools to document assumptions, identify missing evidence and prepare a decision that a responsible person can review.

ENPTESFRDE

Five evidence gates before a pilot

  1. 1. Outcome and owner

    Name the process, site or workload; record the expected outcome, exclusions, accountable owner and decision maker.

  2. 2. Baseline and success measure

    Record current time, cost, incidents or effort. Select a metric and a measurement window before estimating benefits.

  3. 3. Data and security boundaries

    Identify what data may be used, access requirements and a human reviewer. Start with fictional or authorized non-confidential information.

  4. 4. Full costs and approvals

    Separate illustrative calculator output from a supplier-backed quotation. Include labor, travel, taxes, freight, parts and imports when relevant. Field execution requires written client acceptance of scope and price, a PO and a separate Zion operational release.

  5. 5. Acceptance, stop rule and rollback

    Define the test, evidence to capture, who accepts the outcome, when to stop and how to return safely to the previous state.
