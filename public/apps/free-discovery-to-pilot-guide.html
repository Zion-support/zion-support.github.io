1. Keep evidence before changing anything

  1. Save the Discovery report with its date and the process you selected.
  2. Name the process owner and document the systems and data permissions involved.
  3. Measure a baseline: monthly volume, handling time, rework, escalation rate and a relevant quality measure.
  4. Mark each input as measured, estimated or still unknown. Do not present estimates as guaranteed outcomes.

3. Write a reversible pilot brief

  1. Scope: one workflow, one owner, a limited dataset and explicit exclusions.
  2. Success: a measured target compared with baseline, plus a quality and safety threshold.
  3. Controls: human review, access permissions and a written escalation path.
  4. Stop rule: who stops the trial, under what conditions and how to restore the prior workflow.
  5. Review: evaluate evidence after a defined trial period; expand only if the owner approves.
