A small, responsible test

  1. Measure current effort, quality and turnaround before using a tool.
  2. Try one related app with synthetic data; inspect its code and limits.
  3. Compare against the baseline and keep human approval for consequential actions.
  4. Stop if quality, privacy or operational controls fail.
