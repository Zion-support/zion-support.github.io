AI agents and autonomous operations

Eight jobs people ask an agent tool to do, written as notes you fill in. Nothing on this page runs a job, pages a person, or sends a message.

The work

  • Pull request notes — what changed, and who may merge. Nothing is posted.
  • Dispatch orchestrator notes — who gets the next job. No one is paged.
  • Ticket queue notes — what to write before a ticket moves.
  • Voice notes — phrases and languages. Nothing is spoken from the page.

Money and customers

  • Spend notes — what the cloud bill is allowed to do. No change is made.
  • Sales email notes — a draft a person sends. Nothing leaves this site.
  • Growth hub notes — one audience, one offer, one owner for spend.
  • Churn notes — what a save conversation needs. No message is sent.
