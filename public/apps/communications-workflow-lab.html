Turn a communications problem into a small, reviewable pilot. Explore four connected workflows and prepare a written plan for your team.

Available here: guidance and a local pilot worksheet. This page does not route calls, transcribe recordings, connect a CRM or run an AI model.

Before connecting real customer data

  1. Define a baseline: misrouted calls, callback backlog or overdue actions. Choose one success metric and one accountable owner.
  2. Use synthetic or consented data first. Confirm recording consent, retention, access permissions and vendor requirements before sharing customer information.
  3. Require human review, document escalation and a stop rule, and verify integration behavior before expanding the pilot.
