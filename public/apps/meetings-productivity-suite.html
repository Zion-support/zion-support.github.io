Meetings and productivity

Eight jobs people ask a meeting tool to do, written as notes you fill in. Nothing on this page records a call, sets a price, or sends a message.

Before the meeting

  • Meeting cost desk — who is in the room, and what that hour is worth. No calendar is read.
  • Minutes and actions — the decision, the owner, and the date.
  • Voice notes — phrases and languages. Nothing is spoken from the page.

After the meeting

  • Transcript desk — a written record. No audio file is uploaded.
  • Meeting notes — searchable notes with an owner on each line.
  • Translation brief — the written version, checked by a person.
  • Policy notes — consent, retention, and who may open the notes. Not legal advice.
  • Public roadmap — where a decision goes after the meeting.
