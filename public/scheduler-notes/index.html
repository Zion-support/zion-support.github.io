Scheduler notes

A place to write which jobs can wait for a cleaner hour. Nothing on this page is shifted, paused, or metered.

What to write down

  • The job name, the deadline, and who is allowed to move it.
  • The region list you already use. Do not paste a product hostname into the address.
  • Where the carbon note lives. Use the carbon notes. This page does not call a grid API.
