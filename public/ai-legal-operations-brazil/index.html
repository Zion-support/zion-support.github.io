AI in legal operations: from pilot to routine

Generative AI use in Brazilian law firms jumped from 37% in 2024 to 80% in 2025, and reached 100% in firms with more than 700 lawyers (NetDocuments, 2026). Adoption stopped being the differentiator; what separates return from expense is what comes after: governance, training and a documented workflow. Zion builds and operates that layer for legal teams, under the LGPD and OAB guidance.

FAQ

Is AI use by lawyers regulated in Brazil?

The Federal Council of the Bar approved recommendations on generative AI in legal practice in 2024 and launched a National Plan in June 2026. The LGPD applies whenever personal data is processed.

Who is responsible for the legal output?

The lawyer. AI is a support tool; the professional remains responsible for the decision and the document.

Do you sell a packaged legal product?

No. Work is scoped after a free Discovery, and the deliverable is built around your practice.

How do you handle confidentiality?

With approval gates, least-privilege access, audit trails and contractual confidentiality clauses.
