Manual workload cost calculator

Estimate the monthly and annual cost of a manual process before comparing automation options. Enter your own hours and loaded hourly cost to establish a baseline.

1. Define your baseline

The starting values are an example. Replace them with figures for the same process and team. All amounts are in USD; no currency conversion is performed.

Each value must be between 0 and 1,000,000. Decimals are accepted.

2. Estimated workload cost

The calculation runs in this browser tab. This tool does not send or store inputs, connect accounts, create an order, or send email.

How the estimate is calculated

Monthly manual cost = monthly hours × loaded hourly cost.

Annual manual cost = monthly manual cost × 12, assuming the same workload every month.

A small example

At 40 manual hours per month and $45.00 per hour, the baseline is $1,800.00 per month and $21,600.00 per year.

This is the estimated value of the current workload, not measured savings or a return on investment. A return calculation also needs the time actually released, implementation and ongoing costs, and a comparison period. Released time does not automatically reduce payroll or create cash savings.

Build a baseline you can review

  1. Use recent time records or a small measured sample; record which tasks and team members are included.
  2. Use a loaded hourly cost that includes the costs you intend to compare. Keep the scope and currency consistent.
  3. Record these assumptions, test a small change and compare the actual workload afterward.
