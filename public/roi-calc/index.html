How this model works

Monthly recovered hours = team members × hours saved per person per week × 52 ÷ 12. Gross monthly capacity value = hours × fully loaded hourly cost. Net scenario value = gross value across the selected period minus setup and recurring costs.

Interpreting this estimate

Is recovered time the same as cash saved?

No. Recovered capacity has value, but cash savings require a verified reduction in spending or a measurable business outcome.

What does the setup payback figure mean?

Setup cost divided by positive monthly net capacity value. If the scenario has no positive monthly net, payback is undefined.

How should I decide whether to proceed?

Validate your baseline, compare cautious and optimistic assumptions, and test one workflow before committing budget.
