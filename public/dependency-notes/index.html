Dependency notes

A package list a person reviews. Nothing on this page is scanned, scored, or upgraded.

What to write down

  • The name, the version, and who may merge a change.
  • What stays pinned until a person has looked at the diff.
  • If a token is in a lockfile, use the secret notes. This page does not read a repository.
