Marketing automation

A campaign is four facts: who receives it, what is offered, when it sends, and how someone leaves the list. Write those down here. This page does not send mail and does not report open rates.

Audience

Name the list and the date it was last checked for permission. If the source of the address is unknown, do not send.

Offer

One sentence a person can repeat. Link the page the button opens, and check that the page loads.

Stop

Every send needs a working unsubscribe. The owner of replies is a person, not a queue with no name.
