LGPD applied to AI: lawful, auditable and defensible

The LGPD applies to any processing of personal data of people in Brazil, wherever the controller is based. Data does not have to stay in Brazil, but it must be locatable and protected by transfer mechanisms approved by ANPD. For AI systems the practical requirements are: a valid legal basis, human oversight of automated decisions, documented security measures, and a defensible record of how data was used.

What the LGPD requires

Territorial scope

The LGPD reaches processing carried out in Brazil, processing aimed at offering goods or services to people in Brazil, and data collected in Brazil.

Legal bases

Processing must rest on one of the legal bases in Article 7, applied per purpose rather than by default consent.

International transfers

Article 33 allows transfers only through mechanisms approved by ANPD, such as standard contractual clauses.

Automated decisions

AI decisions about individuals require human oversight and the possibility of review, not full automation.

Security measures

Article 46 requires technical and administrative measures sized to the sensitivity and volume of the data.

Data subject rights

Article 18 gives data subjects the right to confirm, access, correct, anonymise, port and delete their data.

FAQ

Does my data have to stay in Brazil?

The LGPD does not require data to be stored exclusively in Brazil. Data must be locatable and protected, and international transfers must use ANPD-approved mechanisms.

Is running inference abroad a transfer?

Yes in practice. If inference happens in a different region from storage, that flow must be covered by a transfer mechanism and documented.

Do you sign DPAs?

We execute data processing agreements and specify the legal basis for any transfer, as required by Article 46 and by procurement practice.

Can you handle sensitive data?

Yes, with consent or another applicable basis, strengthened security measures and a documented RIPD/DPIA for high-risk systems.
