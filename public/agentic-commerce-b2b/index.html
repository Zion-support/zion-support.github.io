What Is Agentic Commerce?

Agentic commerce is the shift where AI agents — not humans — drive buying decisions. For B2B companies, this means your products and content must be discoverable by, and usable by, AI agents.

Based on the 2025–2026 research on agentic AI in B2B (G2, Deloitte, McKinsey), three patterns are emerging:

  • Agent-assisted buyers: Sales reps and buyers use AI agents to research and shortlist.
  • Agentic evaluation: Agents compare, benchmark, and recommend alternatives autonomously.
  • Agent-to-agent commerce: Agents negotiate, order, and manage accounts end-to-end.
