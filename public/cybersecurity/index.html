Cybersecurity

Security work at Zion starts with a named owner, a certificate that matches the hostname, and a written list of what an AI tool can reach. This page does not scan a network and it does not send a test message to anyone.

Start here

  • AI cybersecurity platform — systems, data, and people, written down before a model is connected.
  • Certificate checklist — the name on the certificate matches the name people type.
  • Review desk and scoping worksheet — owner, evidence, and stop rules. No attack text.
  • Safety scope — what the agent is allowed to touch.
  • Incident response and runbook desk — who is called, and what the first hour looks like.
  • Cybersecurity services — the service page for a scoped engagement.
  • Services hub — the official list. Prices stay on Plans.

What a first week usually covers

  1. One hostname and the certificate that should match it.
  2. The systems a model can read or write, and the person who can turn that access off.
  3. Where the log goes, and who reads it the next morning.
  4. A stop rule: what the tool must not do without a person.
