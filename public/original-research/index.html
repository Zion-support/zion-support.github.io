FAQ

Why not just cite someone else's report?

Citing is cheap and replicable. Owning the finding makes you the source others credit, and it is the one asset a competitor cannot reproduce.

Do we need a large sample?

No. A small, well-documented study with a named method is worth more than a large opaque one. We size the study to the claim.

How do you keep it honest?

The methodology is fixed before fielding, the limits are published, and every number traces to a method and a period.

How long does a study take?

A bounded study typically runs from design to publication in weeks, not quarters. The discovery defines the scope.
