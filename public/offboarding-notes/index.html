Offboarding notes

Write the list before the last day. This page does not disable a login, wipe a laptop, or send a notice.

Write down

  • The last day, and the person who confirms the accounts.
  • Email, VPN, source control, and the shared drives to review.
  • Hardware to collect, and where it goes.
  • The knowledge only that person had, and who will cover it next week.
