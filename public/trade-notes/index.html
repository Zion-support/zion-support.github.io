Trade notes

Write the shipment facts on this page's checklist before anyone talks about paperwork. Nothing here is filed, classified, or sent. This is not legal advice.

Write these down

  • Item, quantity, and the commercial name you already use.
  • Origin and destination. Name the country in words. Do not paste a hostname.
  • Documents you already hold, and who is allowed to sign them.
  • The date you need an answer, and who on your side owns the question.
