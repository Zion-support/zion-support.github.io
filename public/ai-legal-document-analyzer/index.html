Legal document note

This path was missing, so the clause reviewer sent people to a 404. The page is a note, not an analyzer. No file is uploaded, no clause is scored, and nothing here is legal advice.

Write down

  • The document name and the one worry (a date, a penalty, or a term you do not understand).
  • Whether counsel has already seen it.
  • Who may change the language, and who may not.

Take the note to counsel or to Discovery. Waiting is an acceptable answer.
