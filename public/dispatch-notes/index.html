Dispatch notes

The older dispatch optimizer does not pick up fixes, and it still links the stuck directory. Plan one visit here. No technician is paged.

Write down

A window on this page is not an assignment.

  • The site, the task, and the skill the visit needs.
  • The window the customer agreed to, and the person who agreed.
  • The part that must already be on the truck, or the visit waits.
  • Who may move the window.
