# App Network Spotlight: IT Asset, License & Compliance Ops (Sep 23, 2026)

Know what you own, when it renews, and prove compliance on demand. The **IT Asset, License & Compliance Ops** pack from the [Zion Tech Group App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/) bundles seven free, open-source AI apps that tame software spend, vendor risk and audit evidence.

## Featured apps
1. **[License Renewal Planner](https://ziontechgroup.com/license-renewal-planner/)** — software licenses, renewals & true-up exposure in one place. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/license-renewal-planner)
2. **[IT Asset Lifecycle Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/it-asset-lifecycle-tracker/)** — purchase-to-disposal asset tracking. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/it-asset-lifecycle-tracker)
3. **[Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/)** — auto-renewal & escalator clause tracking. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/contract-renewal-radar)
4. **[Vendor Risk Scorecard](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-risk-scorecard/)** — score and monitor vendor risk. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-risk-scorecard)
5. **[FinOps Spend Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/finops-spend-analyzer/)** — find wasted SaaS and cloud spend. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/finops-spend-analyzer)
6. **[Data Retention Enforcer](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-retention-enforcer/)** — retention schedules, legal holds & deletion proof. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/data-retention-enforcer)
7. **[Accessibility Statement Gen](https://ziontechgroup.com/accessibility-statement-gen/)** — WCAG-compliant statements & audit evidence. [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/accessibility-statement-gen)

## Why it matters
Unused licenses and surprise auto-renewals drain 20–30% of SaaS budgets. This pack gives IT and procurement teams a zero-cost, zero-install stack to stay ahead — and every app interlinks with the rest of the 700+ app network.
