## How the apps interlink

1. [Meeting Cost Calculator](https://github.com/Zion-support/meeting-cost-calculator) shows what meetings cost; [Meeting Scheduler](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-meeting-scheduler) cuts the waste before it happens.
2. [Meeting Policy Gen](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-meeting-policy-gen) sets consent and retention rules — part of the [AI Trust & Governance Suite](APP_NETWORK_SPOTLIGHT_TRUST_GOVERNANCE.md) — before [Meeting Assistant](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-meeting-assistant) and [Audio Transcriber](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-audio-transcriber) capture anything.
3. [Voice Translator](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-voice-translator) removes language barriers in real time.
4. Notes flow into [Meeting Notes Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-meeting-notes-copilot), and decisions become roadmap items in [Meeting to Roadmap](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-meeting-to-roadmap).
