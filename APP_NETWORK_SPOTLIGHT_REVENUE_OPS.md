## How the apps interlink

1. [Sales Call Analyzer](https://github.com/Zion-support/sales-call-analyzer) extracts intent and objections that sharpen [Pipeline Forecast AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/pipeline-forecast-ai).
2. Qualified deals move to [Proposal Writer AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/proposal-writer-ai) and [Quote Generator AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/quote-generator-ai).
3. Non-standard terms route through [Deal Desk Assistant](https://github.com/Zion-support/deal-desk-assistant) for approval with margin protection.
4. Won deals are watched by [Contract Renewal Radar](https://github.com/Zion-support/contract-renewal-radar); expansion surfaces in [Upsell Signal Scout](https://github.com/Zion-support/upsell-signal-scout).
5. Partner-sourced pipeline is tracked end-to-end in [Partner Referral Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/partner-referral-tracker).
