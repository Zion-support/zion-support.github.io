## How the apps interlink

1. [Sales Intelligence](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-sales-intelligence) surfaces the account; [Sales Call Analyzer](https://github.com/Zion-support/sales-call-analyzer) and [Sales Call Analyst](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-sales-call-analyst) capture the conversation.
2. Signals roll up into [Pipeline Forecast AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/pipeline-forecast-ai) for commit accuracy.
3. At quote time, [Deal Desk Assistant](https://github.com/Zion-support/deal-desk-assistant) guards margins while [Quote Generator AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/quote-generator-ai) prices the deal.
4. [Proposal Writer AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/proposal-writer-ai) and [RFP Builder](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-rfp-builder) turn the win theme into documents.
5. Post-sale handoff flows into the [Customer Experience & Support AI Suite](APP_NETWORK_SPOTLIGHT_SUPPORT_CX.md).
