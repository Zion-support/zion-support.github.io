## What it is
AI that forecasts SLA breaches **before** they happen. SLA Breach Predictor scores every open ticket for breach probability — using SLA clock, priority, parts logistics status and technician capacity — and recommends the cheapest mitigation that protects margin and the client relationship.

## Why it matters for our clients
- Most SLA penalties are predictable days in advance from backlog and parts signals
- Contract-level penalty exposure view helps account managers act before renewal talks
- Built for MSPs, field service organizations and IT desks running NBD/SBD and 24x7 contracts
