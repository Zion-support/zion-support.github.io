# App Network Spotlight — AI Ops, Incident Response & Reliability Suite

**Published 2026-09-24 · Zion Tech Group**

The Zion AI App Network adds a new category: **AI Ops, Incident Response & Reliability** — 18 free, open-source apps that help IT, SRE, MSP and support teams detect, respond to and prevent incidents before they become outages or SLA breaches.

## Why it matters
Downtime and SLA breaches cost enterprises thousands per minute. This suite turns fragmented signals — alerts, logs, tickets, metrics — into guided, executable response.
