The unit of work

Process

one team (for example product support or CS ops) keeping one topic set current from draft to published article

Systems

Operating signal

Stop rule

When a Starter pilot beats another unread wiki

A Starter pilot is the honest next step when the graveyard pattern is already visible and bounded:

  • One team will write and accept. Not “everyone should contribute.” A named editor and a named reviewer for one topic set.
  • One source system is reachable. You are not migrating three help centers, a Confluence swamp, and Slack folklore in the same $2,500 slice.
  • One success metric already exists. You can point at a report or a queue tag you already use. We will not invent a CSAT lift or a deflection percentage to justify the buy.

If those three are true, another strategy workshop will not make articles get read. A bounded Starter — publish path, review queue, stop rule — will. If any of the three is missing, Pay becomes a second wiki nobody reads. That is Consulte or Discovery first.

What Discovery $99 writes

  • Owners and systems for this slice — not a company-wide knowledge transformation.
  • A recommendation: Pay Starter, Consulte a SOW, Growth/retainer, or stop.
  • The stop rule above, in writing, so kickoff cannot smuggle a second product line or a new CMS.

Discovery is not implementation. Implementation is Starter or a scoped SOW. Ongoing coverage is Growth after the model is clear. First-automation framing: Pay vs Consulte for AI automation.

Good after kickoff (no fake math)

  • The process sentence still matches day 10 — same team, same source system, same topic set.
  • Access landed or a named blocker with an owner (CMS, ITSM knowledge space, or repo).
  • Definition of done is operational — a live publish + review path — not “we explored AI search.”
  • Exception queue exists. Humans know which articles automation must not publish alone.

If access is still pending or success was redefined to “we launched a portal,” stop and re-scope. That is Consulte, not a silent expand of Pay.
