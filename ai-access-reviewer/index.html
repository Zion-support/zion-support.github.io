A responsible first step

  1. Agree the owner, scope and approved evidence source; use synthetic examples in public tools.
  2. Have authorized reviewers evaluate necessity, exceptions and separation of duties.
  3. Document an approved decision and rollback plan; real account changes require separate authorization.

Illustrative preparation only: no guaranteed ROI, admission outcome, certification, legal entitlement or automatic security action. Any implementation requires qualified human review and separate approval.
