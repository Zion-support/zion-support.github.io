# Zion App Network Spotlight — Batch 53: Revenue Protection & Operations Signals (Sep 26, 2026)

Eight interlinked revenue-protection apps: **detect → recover → price → watch**.

- **[Revenue Leak Detector](https://ziontechgroup.com/revenue-leak-detector/)** — Finds and stops revenue leakage across billing, contracts and usage before it compounds. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/revenue-leak-detector))
- **[Subscription Dunning Hero](https://ziontechgroup.com/subscription-dunning-hero/)** — Recovers failed subscription payments with smart, branded dunning sequences and retry logic. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/subscription-dunning-hero))
- **[Pricing Signal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/pricing-signal-radar/)** — Tracks competitor pricing moves and market signals, then recommends concrete price actions. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/pricing-signal-radar))
- **[Zion AI Pricing Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-pricing-optimizer/)** — Runs dynamic pricing experiments and elasticity analysis to maximize margin and conversion. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-pricing-optimizer))
- **[Supplier Risk Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/supplier-risk-radar/)** — Monitors supplier financial, geopolitical, ESG and delivery signals with early warnings. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/supplier-risk-radar))
- **[Shipping Delay Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/shipping-delay-alerter/)** — Detects shipment delays early and notifies customers proactively with revised ETAs. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/shipping-delay-alerter))
- **[Vendor Onboarding Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-onboarding-copilot/)** — Streamlines vendor onboarding — document collection, compliance checks, risk screening and approvals. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-onboarding-copilot))
- **[Energy Demand Forecaster](https://ziontechgroup.com/energy-demand-forecaster/)** — Forecasts energy demand by site, meter and weather — budget alerts and peak-shaving recommendations. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-demand-forecaster))
