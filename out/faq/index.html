How fast can you start?

Most engagements begin within 7 days. Advisory and managed work can start sooner when the scope is already written down.

Do you support HIPAA and other regimes?

Yes. We design with privacy-first controls and can support evidence packages for HIPAA, SOC 2, and similar operational requirements.

What does managed AI include?

Monitoring, cost controls, model updates, observability, performance tuning, and incident response for systems already in production.

How do you price work?

Time-and-materials, fixed-price, or retainer. After a short scoping call we return a proposal with the model, milestones, and who does what. Published menu: Discovery $99, Consulting $499, Starter $2,500, Growth $8,000/mo on /plans/.

Do I need AI to work with you?

No. Many clients start with audits, managed IT, or cloud cleanup. We use AI when it shortens analysis or makes operations repeatable.

Which clouds and tools do you integrate?

AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, GitHub, common ITSM/CRM/ERP stacks, and SIEM/EDR. Exact fit is confirmed in discovery.

Which industries do you serve?

Healthcare, financial services, SaaS, logistics, media, energy, education, legal, manufacturing, and internal enterprise IT.

What languages and hours?

English and Portuguese. Coverage is set per contract; email, Calendly, and Google Meet cover most pre-sales questions the same day.

Still have a specific stack question?

Email or call. Most pre-sales questions get a detailed reply within one business hour.
