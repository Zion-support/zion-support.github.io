Portrait consent

A portrait or avatar that shows a real person needs a written yes, and a label when the image is synthetic. This page does not generate an image.

Write down

  • The person, by name.
  • Where the written consent is filed.
  • Every place the image will appear.
  • The disclosure viewers will see.


Do not publish a likeness that could be mistaken for the person speaking or approving something they did not approve.
