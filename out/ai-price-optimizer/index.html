Price note

The older price page talked about live price changes. This page does not change a price, does not watch a competitor, and does not run an experiment.

Write down

  • The price you charge today, and the floor you will not cross.
  • The channel (shelf, quote, or invoice) and the person who may approve a change.
  • The date the current price was last set, if you know it.

A filled note is for a person. It is not a new price and not a forecast.
