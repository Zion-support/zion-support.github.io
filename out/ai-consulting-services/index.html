14|## What AI Consulting Means Here

15|

16|The term "AI consulting" gets used to mean everything from a sales pitch for a platform to a 60-page strategy document that never leaves the shelf. We use it to mean something narrower and more useful:

17|

18|AI consulting is the work of identifying, framing, and executing the right AI and automation opportunities in a specific business — with enough rigor that you can tell whether it worked.

19|

20|That breaks down into four things we actually do:

21|

22|### 1. Strategy and Roadmaps

23|

24|Before any tool is chosen or any workflow is built, someone has to answer the question: what should we do first, what should we not do yet, and how will we know if it worked?

25|

26|A roadmap from Zion is not a list of shiny possibilities. It's a prioritized set of opportunities for your specific operation, ranked by impact and effort, with an honest call on which ones are ready now and which need cleanup first. The roadmap comes with ROI framing for each initiative, so the "what do we do first" decision is a business decision, not a guess.

27|

28|This is the core of the Consulting tier: a structured roadmap plus three working sessions to walk through it.

29|

30|### 2. Vendor and Tool Selection

31|

32|The AI and automation market is full of tools, platforms, and claims. Choosing the wrong one — or choosing one too early, or building on one that doesn't fit the real process — is one of the most expensive mistakes a company can make.

33|

34|We help you select tools and vendors based on the process you're actually trying to improve, not on what's trendy. That means evaluating what you already have and whether it can be connected, what gap a new tool would actually fill, what the integration cost is, and what the ongoing operational cost is. We're tool-agnostic: if connecting what you have solves the problem, we'll say that. If a new tool is warranted, we'll say why and which one fits.

35|

36|This is especially important in a market where vendor lock-in and tool sprawl are real and expensive. A good selection decision now prevents a bad one later.

37|

38|### 3. ROI Analysis

39|

40|Anyone can say an automation will save money. Without a baseline, that's just a claim. ROI analysis at Zion means establishing what the process costs today — in time, errors, delay, or missed opportunity — and then framing what a realistic improvement would look like against that baseline.

41|

42|This is not a guaranteed savings promise. It's a rigorous sketch: here's what we think the opportunity is, here's the assumption set behind it, here's what we'd measure to confirm it, and here's how we'd know if it didn't work. That level of honesty is what makes the ROI analysis useful rather than just flattering.

43|

44|### 4. Implementation Planning

45|

46|Strategy without execution is opinion. Implementation planning is the work of turning a roadmap priority into a real project: scope, tools, connections, dependencies, risks, timeline, and the baseline you'll measure against.

47|

48|This is where the Starter and Growth tiers come in. Starter delivers one complete automation with 30 days of support. Growth delivers ongoing operations with an SLA and up to 5 AI agents. The implementation planning is what makes those engagements land — not just "build an automation," but "build this specific automation on these specific tools, measure it against this baseline, and support it for this long."

111|## AI Consulting vs. AI Implementation: Knowing the Difference

112|

113|It's worth being clear about this, because the market blurs the two.

114|

115|AI consulting is the diagnostic, strategic, and planning work: what should we do, what tools fit, what's the ROI, what's the plan. It produces reports, roadmaps, and decisions.

116|

117|AI implementation is the building work: the automation or AI workflow actually constructed, connected to real tools, tested, and delivered into operation. It produces working systems and measurable results.

118|

119|The best engagements include both, in the right sequence. Consulting before implementation, so the build is based on a real understanding of the process and a real ROI case. Implementation after consulting, so the roadmap turns into something measurable. Discovery is where the two meet for the first time: a real diagnostic that tells you whether implementation is worth pursuing.

120|

121|If a vendor offers you "AI consulting" that's really just a soft pitch for their platform implementation, that's not consulting — that's sales with extra steps. We're clear about the difference, and we're clear about when a consulting engagement leads to an implementation engagement and when it doesn't.
