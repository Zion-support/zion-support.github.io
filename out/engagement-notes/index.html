Engagement notes

The older pulse page leaves this domain for a GitHub Pages host. Use these notes. No survey is sent, and no score is computed.

Write down

A note is not a sentiment score.

  • The team, and the person who will read the note.
  • What changed since the last conversation, in one or two sentences.
  • The question you will ask in person, and the date.
  • What you will not infer from a blank response.
