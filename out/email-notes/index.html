Email notes

A mail brief you fill in yourself. Nothing is sent, sequenced, or measured from this page.

What to write down

  • The list name, and the date a person last checked that the addresses were permitted.
  • One offer, and the page the button opens. Open that page before you approve the send.
  • Who may send, and where a person replies. An unsubscribe link has to work before the first message.
  • What a tool may draft, and what it may not send.
