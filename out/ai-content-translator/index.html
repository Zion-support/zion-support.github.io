Translation brief

The older translator page sent people to hosts that do not present a valid certificate, and it listed mailboxes this company does not use. Use this brief instead. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is translated on this page.

Write down

  • The source line, in the language you already have.
  • The name, price, or legal line that must not change.
  • The language you need, and the channel (subtitle, email, or page).
  • The person who approves the file before it is published.



A filled brief is a note for a person. It is not a translation, not a likeness, and not proof that a file is ready to publish.
