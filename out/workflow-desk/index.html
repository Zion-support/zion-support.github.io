Workflow desk

Use this desk before you draw a workflow. The builder on this site is linked below. This page does not execute a step.

Write down

  • The trigger, in one sentence.
  • The branch: what happens when the check fails.
  • The person who can stop the run.


A step that changes a customer, a payment, or access waits for the person named above.
