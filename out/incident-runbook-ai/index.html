Incident runbook notes

An outline a person writes before an incident. Nothing is generated, paged, or sent.

What to write down

  • Who is on the first call, by name, from your own roster.
  • How you will tell customers. The status note is the sentence. This page does not post it.
  • What "back to normal" means for this service, in your words.
  • Where the longer desk lives: the runbook desk and the incident commander desk.
