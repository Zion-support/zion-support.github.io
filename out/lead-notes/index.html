Lead notes

Fields a person can read before a conversation. Nothing is scored, ranked, or sent from this page.

What to write down

  • The account name, and who owns the next conversation.
  • The source you already have: a form, a referral, or a list you typed.
  • What would make you decline, in one sentence.
  • The page you will send. Use the sales email notes. This page does not send it.
