DevOps notes

A release list a person fills in. Nothing on this page is built, deployed, or scanned. The older page on this address loaded scripts that do not exist.

What to write down

  • What is about to ship, and who can roll it back.
  • Where logs and a status note live after the change.
  • Which customer-facing page must still open: chat, CRM, invoice, analytics, and security notes.
  • The incident note to open if the change breaks a job.
