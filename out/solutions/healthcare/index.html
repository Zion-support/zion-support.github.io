Healthcare

Clinical and revenue-cycle operations that need an audit trail, not a chatbot in front of the EHR.

What a first engagement covers

  • Access reviews for systems that already store patient or member records.
  • Ticket triage for clinic, imaging, and revenue-cycle support, with a named owner.
  • A cloud-cost review that does not touch clinical uptime or the system of record.
  • A written decision on whether an agent belongs in the workflow at all.

How the first week is scoped

List the systems that already store records, who may see a summary, and which notes never leave the system of record. A model summary is not a clinical decision, a diagnosis, or a coverage determination. The first week names one queue, one reviewer, and the fields that stay inside the record system.

What to bring to Discovery

  • The queue you want mapped, and the person who closes it today.
  • Which system is the record, and which tools are only copies.
  • The access review you already run, even if it is a spreadsheet.
