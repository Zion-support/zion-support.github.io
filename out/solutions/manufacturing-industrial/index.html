Manufacturing and industrial

Maintenance windows, quality holds, and the IT that keeps the line talking to the plant.

What a first engagement covers

  • One maintenance or quality workflow, with the system of record named.
  • Patch windows operations will actually accept.
  • Monitoring scoped to a site, not a plant-wide platform pitch.
  • A readout that says build, wait, or do not build.

How the first week is scoped

Start with one line, one hold code, or one maintenance queue. The first week names the system that already records the event and the person who releases the hold. A summary on this site does not change a machine.

What to bring to Discovery

  • The work order or hold you want shorter.
  • Who is allowed to release it.
  • The maintenance window the plant will actually give IT.
