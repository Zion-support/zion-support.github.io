SEO brief

The older SEO optimizer still sends people through a directory that does not pick up fixes. Use this brief. Nothing is crawled, scored, or published.

Write down

A brief is the page you would edit, not a rank forecast.

  • The query a person actually types, in their words.
  • The URL you would change, and the sentence that should answer the query.
  • The proof on that page today, and the proof that is still missing.
  • Who may publish, and which claims stay out.
