The path, end to end

A typical engagement moves through four steps. Each step has a free tool or a priced plan attached.

Step 1 — Discover
Run a free tool (ROI calculator, health check, workflow builder) or book Discovery $99. Either way you get a written read on one process.
Step 2 — Decide
The read says "build", "wait", or "don't automate". If build, the next SKU is Consulting $499 or Starter $2,500 — not a catalog of extras.
Step 3 — Deliver
One automation or agent shipped (Starter), a multi-agent team (Consulting → Growth), or field/hardware scoped and dispatched.
Step 4 — Operate
Ongoing operations, SLA, and FinOps under Growth $8,000/mo — or a one-off field visit. Scope closes with the team, not on the site.
