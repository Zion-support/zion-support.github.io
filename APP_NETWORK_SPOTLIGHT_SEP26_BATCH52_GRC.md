Eight interlinked GRC apps: **assess → evidence → watch → screen**.

- **[Managed IT Health Check](https://ziontechgroup.com/managed-it-health-check/)** — Scored self-assessment of infrastructure, security and operations maturity with benchmark comparisons. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/managed-it-health-check))
