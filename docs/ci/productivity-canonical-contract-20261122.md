`test_pages_publisher_freshness.py` executes the complete 15-test `PublicationContracts` suite owned by #71963. It does not install a second copy of the old GitHub-script guards. These tests establish the canonical source/provenance contract; they do not claim a current health observation, provider result or receipt from production.
