# Spotlight — Customer Success AI Suite

**Keep every customer, grow every account.** Eight free, interconnected AI apps from the [Zion Tech Group App Network](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network) that cover the full post-sale journey: onboarding → health → sentiment → churn prevention → expansion.

## The suite

1. **[Onboarding Journey Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/onboarding-journey-tracker/)** — track onboarding milestones, time-to-value and stalled accounts. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/onboarding-journey-tracker))
2. **[Customer Health Scorer](https://ziontechgroup.com/customer-health-scorer/)** — unified health scoring from usage, support and billing signals. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/customer-health-scorer))
3. **[Support Sentiment Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/support-sentiment-monitor/)** — real-time sentiment across tickets, chats and calls. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/support-sentiment-monitor))
4. **[NPS Insight Engine](https://ziontechgroup.com/nps-insight-engine/)** — NPS & CSAT analysis with AI themes, drivers and follow-up workflows. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/nps-insight-engine))
5. **[Churn Risk Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/churn-risk-radar/)** — predict churn risk with save plays and exec alerts. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/churn-risk-radar))
6. **[Upsell Signal Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/upsell-signal-scout/)** — detect upsell & expansion signals from product usage. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/upsell-signal-scout))
7. **[Voice of Customer Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/voice-of-customer-hub/)** — reviews, tickets, surveys & social mentions unified with AI themes. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/voice-of-customer-hub))
8. **[Support Deflection Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/support-deflection-analyzer/)** — measure and grow ticket deflection from docs and bots. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/support-deflection-analyzer))

## How they interlock

Onboarding Journey Tracker feeds early-life signals into Customer Health Scorer; Support Sentiment Monitor and NPS Insight Engine enrich the score; Churn Risk Radar turns low scores into save plays; Upsell Signal Scout turns high scores into expansion pipeline; Voice of Customer Hub and Support Deflection Analyzer close the loop across every channel.
